# A Letter to Hosilla from Staunton Vhane

*Found among the effects of a demonic agent in Kenabres — a directive from Staunton Vhane, signed only "S. V.," instructing a subordinate named Hosilla to lie low among the city's mongrelmen until the wardstone's fall makes her useless to him.*

<!-- letter -->

**Hosilla**,

You will remain, for the time being, in **Kenabres**, but know this: the city's days are numbered. Seek a place of safety — the underground den of your mongrel lackeys should suffice to keep you safe from the devastation to come. I shall assume command of **Drezen** shortly, and once **Vorlesh** has finished with the wardstone and **Kenabres** is no longer of interest to us, you are to return to my side.

Excellent news regarding the salvage of **Yaniel's sword** from the museum as well — bring it with you, for I believe this weapon could be quite useful once we corrupt it. Before you leave for **Drezen**, stop by the three safe houses (**Nyserian Manor**, **Topaz Solutions**, and the **Tower of Estrod**) — the passphrase remains "I've new material for the archives," for now — to ensure no evidence remains behind.

May Lord **Deskari** and Lord **Baphomet** watch over you!

**S. V.**
