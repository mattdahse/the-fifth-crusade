# Canonical Monster References

This directory is the reference library for **how the campaign's monsters look**, parallel to
[`../characters/CANON.md`](../characters/CANON.md) for named people. Where the stock art that
ships with a module reads as too cartoonish for the chronicle, a fresh reference is painted here
in the house look — [`../bible/04-visual-style-guide.md`](../bible/04-visual-style-guide.md) —
built from the module's own art as the anatomical source, not from a text description alone. Once
a monster type appears in an actual session, its portrait here is the likeness reference for any
chapter illustration that includes it, the same way a character portrait is.

These are **species/type references, not individuals** — unlike the Cast, a row here has no name
or personality, only the body plan a fresh render must match.

**The one exception is a named Abyssal power.** A demon lord *is* an individual, so its row works
like a Cast portrait rather than a body plan: the likeness belongs to that being alone and must be
preserved from picture to picture exactly as [`../characters/CANON.md`](../characters/CANON.md)
requires. Such rows are marked *(an individual)*.

## Registry

| Monster | Reference | Likeness anchors (keep these constant) |
|---|---|---|
| **Ghoul** | *(no portrait yet — body plan established by [`../images/the-bell-that-never-rang.webp`](../images/the-bell-that-never-rang.webp), Book II Ch. VI)* | Gaunt, stooped, roughly man-sized undead. **Hairless**, with tight grey-tan hide drawn over the bones so the ribs, shoulder blades and joints stand out hard beneath it. A bald skull-like head with a **heavy jaw hinged far too wide**, crowded with long uneven teeth, and small **dead black eyes** sunk in dark sockets. Long thin arms ending in **long clawed fingers**, and a stooped forward-leaning posture. Wears the **rotted tatters of ordinary clothing** — dark rags, scraps of belt and harness — never armour and never a weapon; they fight with claw and bite. **They are corpse-coloured and dry, not green, not glossy and not skeletal** — hide over bone, not bare bone. |
| **Neh-thalggu** ("brain collector") | [`neh-thalggu.webp`](neh-thalggu.webp) | Large spider-like aberration. Bulbous stone-grey pitted head-mass atop a broader rust-red chitinous abdomen; eight long segmented rust-red legs, each joint marked by a small glinting pale-green gem-like growth, ending in curved dark claws. A tight cluster of swollen translucent pale blue-white bulbous sacs sits on top of the head-mass, glowing faintly — the harvested brains. A ring of small pale green-white eye-like beads circles the base of the head at the mouth. Hanging from beneath the head is a mass of thin, dexterous, reddish tentacles — **not a single rigid proboscis** — dextrous enough to grip and manipulate held objects; render at least one tentacle actually holding something when the scene calls for it. Cold pale-blue light from the brain-sac cluster is its natural single light source in a dim scene. |
| **Kalavakus demon** | [`kalavakus.webp`](kalavakus.webp) | Large bipedal demon. Deep indigo-purple, muscular, corded flesh with a pale tan-cream patch of hide across the chest and belly. Digitigrade, reverse-jointed goat-like hind legs ending in broad clawed feet. A cluster of at least four or five long curling ram-like horns of different lengths sweeps back from the crown of the head, framing a heavy brow; wide flat demonic snout, mouth full of crooked sharp teeth. Both hands end in three or four long curved black claws; muscular shoulders and forearms. A long thick tail, ridged with small bony spikes or a club-like knot near the tip. Wears nothing but a simple worked-leather belt or loincloth — no other clothing or armor. |
| **Coloxus demon** | [`coloxus.webp`](coloxus.webp) | Man-sized humanoid demon with a huge fly's head — bulbous multifaceted compound eyes taking up most of the skull, coarse bristling hair-like spines around the crown and jaw, a long downward-curving proboscis-like snout, small mandible-like mouthparts. Two pairs of veined, translucent insect wings fold down its back. Wears an ornate high-collared scholar's coat in tarnished oxblood red edged with dulled gold scrollwork, over a cream ivory undershirt with full sleeves, a round medallion at the throat, dark leather gloves and knee boots. **One hand is insectoid, clawed and chitinous; the other is human-gloved** — keep that asymmetry. The insectoid hand holds an ornate ceremonial rod tipped with a glinting gem; the gloved hand holds a long unrolled parchment scroll, its loose end sealed with a wax seal on a cord. A creature of paperwork and bargains — render it holding both scroll and rod whenever the scene allows. |
| **Nocticula**, demon lord of the Midnight Isles *(an individual — see the note above)* | [`nocticula.webp`](nocticula.webp) | Tall, slender, regal demoness, human-shaped from the thighs up. Cold grey-white skin, a long sharp beautiful face, black eyes, dark oxblood lips, long straight blue-black hair past her shoulders. **The crown of horns is the most recognisable thing about her** — a radiating fan of roughly ten long tapering glossy black horns sweeping outward and back from brow and temples like a sunburst diadem, each banded and tipped with dull gold, the two outermost hooking downward. Enormous outspread bat wings, far wider than her body, the membranes deep crimson-oxblood and covered **edge to edge in close rows of glowing red Abyssal script** burning through the skin of the wing like embers seen through cloth; wing bones and leading edges are tarnished bronze-brown and end in hooked claws. She wears a close-fitting garment of black shadow-silk — narrow jagged ribbon-like panels and straps ending in torn banner edges, matte black, **no jewellery and no armour**. In one raised hand, a small ornate **hand crossbow** of dark wine-red lacquered wood and bone, recurved limbs, a black bolt already nocked. From mid-thigh down her legs are **blackened volcanic stone** — a rough basalt crust split by glowing orange-white molten fissures, tapering to blocky columnar feet, molten light pooling on the ground beneath her. Several long dark-brown **barbed tendrils**, like thorned vines, coil up around each stone leg and lash outward into hooked tips. Her own lava-stone legs are her natural single light source in a dark scene. **Watch the lower body** — asked for loosely, the two stone legs fuse into one molten column rising out of a vent, or vanish under a floor-length gown; say *two separate, distinct legs with a visible gap between them*, and put `fused or merged legs, a single column instead of legs, a floor-length skirt covering the legs` in the `Avoid:` line. |
