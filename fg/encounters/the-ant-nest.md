Four drones, in the nest chamber at the west end of the tunnel under
[the Sarkorian House](../maps/the-sarkorian-house.md).

## Foes

- 4x fiendish_giant_ant_drone

## Running it

Four CR 1 drones is 1,600 XP, which is a **hard** fight for four 1st-level characters and an
outright dangerous one if they walk into the chamber and let all four reach them at once. That is
the intended shape, and the map is what makes it survivable: the tunnel is one creature wide, and
a party that fights at the door — or better, in the tunnel — takes them one or two at a time
instead of four abreast.

So the interesting decision happens before initiative. Opening the hidden door is loud, and the
drones come to the noise. A party that opens it and backs into the cellar fights on ground it
chose, with a doorway to hold and a stair at its back. A party that opens it and pushes down the
tunnel toward the chamber is choosing the drones' ground, in the dark, at speed 50 against theirs.

- **They do not spread out and they do not flank cleverly.** Mindless. They take the shortest
  line to the nearest warm thing and bite it.
- **Nothing here checks morale.** No drone flees, and killing three does not discourage the
  fourth.
- **The clutch is the pressure.** Drones nearest the egg heaps will not leave them, so a party
  that pushes into the chamber is fighting creatures that will not chase them back out — which
  is a way out, if anyone thinks of it.
- **Fire and cold are the wrong answers.** Resistance 5 to both blunts exactly the damage a
  1st-level party is most likely to bring, and *burning hands* off the Hookhand Acolyte is not
  the tool here either.

**If the party is three, or beaten up from the house above**, cut it to three drones and let the
fourth be heard digging somewhere out of sight. That keeps the nest feeling occupied without
adding a body to the fight.
