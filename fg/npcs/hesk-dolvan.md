## Description

> A heavy man past fifty in a good coat gone shiny at the elbows, with a scrap merchant's hands
> and a scrap merchant's way of looking at you — pricing you. He does not pick up the axe behind
> the counter until he has finished deciding whether he needs to.

## Special abilities

**Adept spells, CL 3rd, concentration +5.** He is a scrap dealer who learned four prayers, and
they are exactly the four a man protecting a house would learn.

## Tactics

- **He fights in the doorway to the back rooms** and nowhere else. Everything he does is about
  what is behind him.
- **He opens with *cause fear*** on whoever looks most dangerous, then *bless* if he has a round
  to spare and his people are still up.
- **He does not run**, and this is the problem with him. If the party breaks the yard and the
  shop, Hesk is still standing in that doorway.

## Roleplaying

He took the maze twenty years ago in Kenabres, for reasons he no longer examines, and he has been
quietly useful to the Ivory Labyrinth ever since — a yard where things can be stored, a cellar
with a door out of the city, a name nobody in the watch has written down.

**He believes he has kept his family out of it.** He has not. His wife wears the mark, his
children have been told what the tunnel is for, and the six men down the stairs sleep in his
cellar. Say this to him and watch it land, because he has never once had it put to him plainly.

**He will trade his own life for theirs, immediately and without negotiating.** That is the whole
of him, and a party that offers it gets the intel, the tunnel, and a man who walks to the watch
house on his own feet. A party that kills its way through the house gets the same intel out of a
drawer and never learns what any of it was for.
