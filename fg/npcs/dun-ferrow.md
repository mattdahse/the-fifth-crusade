## Description

> A big young man with a sack of meal on one shoulder and a rope sling across his chest, the kind
> of porter who carries whatever anyone pays him to carry. He is broad and slow and gentle, and he
> is looking at the crate by the pump with his mouth open.

None.

## Tactics

- **He drops the sack and runs, the wrong way.** Roll randomly for which end of the street, and
  he goes for it even if there is a centipede in between. He is the likeliest person on the map
  to be bitten, and the likeliest to be bitten twice.
- The sack of meal he drops splits across his square: that square is **difficult terrain** for the
  rest of the fight.

## Roleplaying

**He is the porter the story record means** when it says a porter brought back from dying does
not forget. Dun is not clever and knows it, and he has never in his life been important enough for
anyone to save. If somebody does, he decides on the spot that he belongs to them.

- **Afterwards** he turns up wherever the company is, carrying things for them unasked and refusing
  payment. The next time the party needs a man on a door who will not ask questions, he is the one
  who volunteers, and he means it.
- **What he wants:** to be useful to somebody who will tell him what to do.
- He is not Mira's porter, the one who brings the message about the nightingale. He will be very
  confused to meet him.
