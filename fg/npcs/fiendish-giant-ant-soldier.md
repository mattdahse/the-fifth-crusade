## Description

> It is the size of a pony and it does not care that you are there in any way you can use. The
> carapace is the dark red-brown of dried blood, dull as horn, scratched down the flanks from
> years of squeezing through its own tunnels. The mandibles are serrated and they are already
> open. Behind them the abdomen curls forward, and the sting at the end of it is wet. Its eyes
> catch a cold amber light, and there is nothing in this hole that is casting it.

## Special abilities

- **Grab** on the bite. Free grapple on a hit, no attack of opportunity, and it can take
  &#8211;20 on the check to keep stinging while it holds someone.
- **Poison** on the sting. Fort DC 14, 1d2 Strength per round for 4 rounds, cured by one save.
- **Resist cold 5, fire 5**, from the fiendish template.
- **Mindless.** Intelligence is absent, not low. Immune to every mind-affecting effect, and it
  cannot be frightened, charmed, bluffed, intimidated or reasoned with.

## Tactics

A soldier's whole purpose is the clutch, and it fights like something with no reason to survive
the encounter.

**Full attack, every round it can.** Bite and sting together is the point of this creature: the
bite grabs, and the sting then lands on someone who is not going anywhere. A soldier that gets a
grapple does not let go and does not reposition — it stands where it is and stings the same
target until one of them stops.

**Speed 50 feet** decides where the fight happens. It moves two squares for the party's one in a
corridor built at its own width, so retreating down the tunnel is not a retreat.

**It will not leave the clutch.** Soldiers fighting within reach of the egg heaps hold that
ground and do not pursue back up the tunnel. That is the party's way out, if anyone notices.

**No morale, ever.** Nothing here checks, flees, surrenders, or is discouraged by the deaths of
the other three.

## Roleplaying

There is none, and that is the point — worth making the table feel. **Nothing about this creature
can be bribed, threatened, parleyed with, or turned.** A party that has spent the campaign
handling people, buying off squatters and reading who wants an excuse to quit, arrives here and
finds every one of those tools dead in their hands.

The fiendish part is not a personality either. Something out of the Worldwound got into this hive
some generations back, and what came out burns cold and no longer entirely belongs on this plane.
It still digs. It still tends the clutch. It is still an ant, and that is the unsettling part
rather than a mercy.
