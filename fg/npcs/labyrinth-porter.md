## Description

> A woman in a porter's leather yoke with a pad stitched over each shoulder, a sack of grain
> halfway onto it and her hands still on the sack. She looks at you, then at the sack, and puts
> the sack down very carefully, as though it might be evidence.

## Tactics

**They do not fight.** They were hired to carry. A porter who is threatened puts down whatever she
is holding and puts her hands where they can be seen, and if the party is between her and the way
out she sits down against the wall until somebody tells her what happens now.

## Roleplaying

The Keeper hires them through Hesk Dolvan, a sack of grain a night, and they believe, because it
is convenient to, that he is a grain merchant avoiding the market's fees. They have seen the maze
on the initiates' throats and the pit in the midden, and they have decided not to know what either
means, which is a skill the South Bank teaches young.

- **Can they be bought?** For the same wage, instantly and with relief: a sack of grain a night to
  carry the grain *back* is the best offer anybody has made them this year.
- **What they know:** that the loads go west through the Gate at dusk; that the Keeper and the
  Horn have not spoken in three weeks; that the Horn is kinder; that the rats have eaten more than
  has gone west, and that the Keeper writes it down anyway.
