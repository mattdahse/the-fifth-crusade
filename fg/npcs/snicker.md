## Familiar abilities

**Not a pet and not a combatant.** Snicker has 3 hit points and a bite that does 1d3-4, which is
to say nothing. What he is for is the four abilities every familiar has and the one that is his.

- **Alertness** — Theep gains the Alertness feat while Snicker is within arm's reach.
- **Improved evasion** — a successful Reflex save takes no damage; a failed one takes half.
- **Share spells** — Theep may cast a personal-range spell on Snicker instead of himself.
- **Empathic link** — emotion, not words, up to a mile. Theep knows when Snicker is frightened,
  and Snicker is frightened more often than Theep is.
- **The monkey's own gift: Theep gains a +3 bonus on Acrobatics checks.**

**His hit points are half his master's**, so he goes up and down as Theep does. At 1st level that
is **3**, and a single hit from almost anything kills him. Replacing him costs **200 gp per wizard
level** and eight hours, which at 1st level is more money than Theep has ever held.

## Tactics

**He does not fight.** Sent into a room he will climb to the highest point in it and stay there,
and Theep will know what he feels but not what he sees. Used well he is a tripwire, a lockpick
carrier, a way to get a torch onto a high ledge, and an early warning that something in the dark
is looking back.

Used badly he is 200 gp and a permanent hole in his master's Acrobatics.

## Roleplaying

**Snicker is old.** Theep did not summon a familiar so much as inherit one, and he has never said
from whom. The monkey is grey around the muzzle and stiff in the mornings and has clearly done
this before with somebody else.

**He is the only creature Theep is gentle with**, which is the most useful thing about him at the
table. A man who is calm about destroying things and careful about one small animal is legible in
a way that a man who is calm about everything is not. Play the contrast: Theep will step over a
body without looking down and then check the monkey's foot for a splinter.

**He does not like the smell of the Ivory Labyrinth's people** and will not go near them, which is
worth a free warning the first time it happens and nothing thereafter.
