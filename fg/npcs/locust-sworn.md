## Description

> Younger than the Labyrinth's men and worse fed. Studded leather, a spear held two-handed, and
> the same brand on the neck that their man carries — but theirs is newer, and only done once,
> and it has not healed clean.
>
> They stand where Corwin puts them and they do not look at the hall.

## Tactics

- **They stand where they are told**, in front of Corwin, and they hold rather than advance.
- **They do not break when he is alive.** They will take the fight past the point where a
  mercenary would leave, because leaving is not a thing that has been available to them.
- **They break instantly when he falls.** No morale, no rally, no second wind — they drop the
  spears and run for the breach, and a party that lets them go has made a decision that will come
  back in a later session.

## Roleplaying

They are not fanatics. They are two young men who were starving in Drezen's outer wards in the
weeks after the crusade took the city - when there were suddenly more mouths than bread - and were
fed by a big calm man who told them there was a purpose in it. The brand came a few weeks later,
and it was explained as a kindness.

**They can be talked to, and they are the softest thing in this building.** Neither has ever been
asked a question by someone who was not Corwin. A party that separates one of them — in the store
room, in the breach, anywhere out of his sight — will get the layout of the house, the size of
the Labyrinth's post, and the fact that there is something behind the rubble in the north-east
room, in about a minute.

**What they will not do is contradict him in front of him.** Ask in the hall and you get nothing.

**They are afraid of the sealed room** and cannot say why with any precision, which is worse.
Corwin has told them what is behind it and they have decided to believe him, and neither of them
sleeps in the table room any more.
