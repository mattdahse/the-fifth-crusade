## Description

> Something the size of a rolled cloak lets go of the roof of the cavern and falls on the
> lantern-bearer without a sound. It is all leather and gripping muscle, the colour of wet stone,
> and it wraps the head first.

@link ability: grab | Grab (Ex)
@link ability: darkmantle_constrict | Constrict (Ex)

## Special abilities

- **Grab** on the slam, then **constrict 1d4+4** every round it holds on. A grappled character
  with one of these over their head is suffocating on the initiative count, not eventually.
- **Blindsight 90 ft.** — putting out the lights does not help. It is the light-bearer's problem
  that darkness is not a solution here.
- **Stealth +10** and it hangs still. Spotting one before it drops is a Perception check against
  a creature actively pretending to be a lump of rock.

## Tactics

**They drop, they grab, and they do not manoeuvre.** A darkmantle that has hold of a head stays
on that head. One that misses flops, is briefly clumsy on the ground (speed 20), and climbs for
the ceiling again if it can.

**Fight them in waves, not as a mob.** They do not all release at once — see the encounter.

## Roleplaying

None; they are animals with an ambush and no plan. What they *mean* is the point: the cult keeps
a rope and a bolt-hole on the far side of this cavern and still sends people through it two at a
time, carrying torches, quickly. That is the tell the party should read.
