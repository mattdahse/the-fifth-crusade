## Description

> A broad, grey-bearded man of about fifty-five squatting on his heels at the edge of a rug
> covered in things that used to be broken: hinges, a kettle, a lock with a new key tied to it,
> a mended lantern, a row of old tools gone brown and oiled back to life. He has one eye and a
> leather patch over the other, and he watches the alleys the way you do when you have spent a
> long time underground.

## Special abilities

None.

## Tactics

- **He holds his rug.** Anything that comes onto it gets the pry bar, centipede or not. He does
  not leave it to chase anything, and he does not go to help anyone.
- **He is one of only two bystanders who can actually hurt a centipede**, and he will do it only
  on his own square of cobbles.
- He is not afraid. He is annoyed.

## Roleplaying

**He dug under Drezen's walls in the taking this Neth**, a sapper with the crusade, and when the
city fell he stayed to dig out its ruins instead. Everything on his rug came out of a house nobody
has claimed yet. He mends it and sells it, and he treats anyone who asks where it came from as
having asked a stupid question.

**He knows the centipedes.** *"South Bank cellars. There's nests down there the size of a wagon
bed."* He says it to whoever is nearest when the lid comes off, and if anyone asks him afterwards
he says more: which cellars, which streets, and that he stopped digging on the south side a
fortnight ago because of what he found. That is a lead toward the South Bank Job, and it costs a
silver or a drink.

**He and Riven will get on**, because they notice the same things. If Riven is crouched at his rug
when it begins, Wat says *"Alley's there, if you're going,"* without looking up.

- **Bribe?** Yes, and happily: information is stock like anything else. He is the one professional
  in the street.
- **What he wants:** to be left alone to work, and paid.
