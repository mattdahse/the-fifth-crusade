## Description

> For a moment there is nothing on the acolyte's shoulder. Then there is: a thing the size of a
> cat, grey-green and warty as a toad, crouched there with its long fingers knotted in her hair.
> It has a hooked nose, a mouth too wide for its face, two little curled horns, and a pair of
> leathery wings folded tight like an umbrella in a hurry. Its eyes are yellow and very pleased
> with themselves. It has been there the whole time.
>
> It notices that you can see it, and its face goes through a short, rapid sequence of
> looks, ending on wounded innocence.

## Special abilities

- **Invisibility at will** (self only). She spends nearly all her time invisible on Ysolde's left
  shoulder, close enough to whisper. She drops it only to attack, which ends it, and she can put
  it back on as a standard action.
- **Voice of the Brood.** The thing that matters, and it is on its own record. The fiendish ants
  in this hive do what *she* says, not what Ysolde says.
- **Poison** on the claws: Fort DC 13, 1d2 Dexterity per round for 6 rounds, cured by one save.
- **Cause fear**, once a day, 30-foot burst, Will DC 11. She saves it for when the company is
  winning.
- **DR 5/cold iron or good.** **Kora's cold iron dagger** goes straight through it. Say so the
  first time it bites, because it is the one weapon in the company built for this.
- **Fast healing 2.** If she gets away, she comes back whole.
- **Change shape**, bat or monstrous centipede. In the hive she goes about as a centipede.

## Tactics

**She is a coward and a schemer, and she fights like one.**

- **She never fights fair if she can avoid fighting.** Invisible on Ysolde's shoulder, she turns
  Ysolde's orders to the ants into real ones, and leaves the squatters and Ysolde to do the work.
- **If she must, she claws from invisibility** at whoever is weakest, takes the poison's effect as
  her win, and flies up out of reach to go invisible again.
- **When the fight turns, she uses cause fear**, and then she **bargains**.
- **When she loses, she sells Ysolde.** Without hesitating, and with a speech about how she was a
  prisoner here too. See **Roleplaying**.
- **When all else fails, she flees**: up the cold chimney in the great hall, then down the
  bore-hole to her queen. Fast healing does the rest. A Nib who gets away is a Nib who comes back.

## Roleplaying

### What she was

**She was Ostrander Gell's familiar.** Gell was the Labyrinth's arcanist, the one who set up the
counting-post, and Nib served him the way a quasit serves anyone: obediently, resentfully, and
with an eye out for a better offer. A little over a year ago Gell found the young queen under his
house and wanted to study her. Nib encouraged this. She went down the bore-hole with him to show
him the best place to stand.

He did not come back up. A familiar bound to a dead master is free, and Nib had been studying the
queen too.

### What she is doing

**The ants answer her.** She has the queen's ear in the only sense a mindless queen has one, and
the soldiers take her direction as though it came from the clutch (see **Voice of the Brood**).
She discovered she could do this about a week after Gell died, and she has been building on it
ever since.

**She needed hands.** A quasit cannot carry a muster list, hold a mace, give orders to men, or
feed them to anything. So she found **Ysolde Karn**, the post's adept, who prayed to Deskari in
the night where Gell could not hear, and who was ready to believe she was chosen. Nib gave her a
crown, a destiny and an invisible voice in her ear. Ysolde did the rest.

**The men are food.** Every man Ysolde has sent down the hatch has gone to grow the brood, and
the brood is growing. **The tunnel is the dream.** A passage dug east under the Marchlands all
the way to Drezen's walls, for the Locust Host to come up through when it is ready. It will take
years. Nib does not care. She is going to arrive in the Worldwound at the head of a swarm big
enough for Deskari to notice her name, and the Lord of the Locust Host is going to raise her out
of the ranks of the little demons forever. She talks about this a great deal when nobody she
respects is listening, which is always.

### How to play her

**Petty, vain, clever, cowardly, and very funny right up until she is not.** She is the most
intelligent thing in the house and she knows it. She is also three feet tall with a bad
complexion, and she knows that too, and it makes her vicious.

- **She talks to Ysolde constantly**, in a whisper nobody else can hear. Ysolde answers aloud.
  To anyone watching, the acolyte has long reasonable conversations with nobody.
- **She is a flatterer.** To Ysolde she is *my lady, chosen of the Host, Crown of the Swarm*. To
  the company, when she has to deal, she is all charm. She will call Riven *cousin*, because of
  the horns, and be genuinely hurt when it does not work.
- **She will bargain**, and it is a real negotiation. What she offers, in order: her silence;
  Ysolde, bound and delivered; the way to the queen up the bore-hole; and last and most
  reluctantly, **where Gell's satchel is hidden**, because she has been saving that for herself.
  What she wants: to leave, alive, with the hive. She will never actually give up the hive.
- **She cannot be trusted with anything.** Any deal she makes she breaks the moment it costs her
  nothing to break it. A party that makes a deal with her should be made to feel very clever for
  about one scene.

### The voice in the dark

**This is what happened to Garth.** On nights when she was bored, Nib went among the sleeping
men in the pallet room and whispered to them: about the hatch, about the hum under the floor,
about which of them would go down next. It is the kind of thing a quasit does for fun. Several of
the men who argued with Ysolde about the hatch were arguing because Nib had spent a week making
sure they would. **Garth heard her for three weeks.** It is why he believes something small and
invisible rides on people's shoulders. He is right.

### If she is caught

Bound, cornered, or with Kora's dagger at her throat, she is the most cooperative creature in the
Marchlands. She will tell the company everything: about Gell, about the ants, about the crown.
**Every word is true.** She has decided that the truth is what hurts Ysolde most, and that is the
best revenge left to her.

She will also, at the end, ask very sweetly whether they are going to let her go. **That is the
company's decision.** A quasit set free goes back to the hive. A dead one, on the Material Plane,
goes back to the Abyss in disgrace, which is the thing she fears most in the world.
