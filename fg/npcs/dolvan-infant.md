## Why this is a token at all

**It marks a square.** That is the whole purpose and it is worth being direct about: a
*burning hands* cone laid through the children's doorway, a *colour spray*, a flask of oil, or a
summoned animal sent in ahead covers this square, and a GM should be able to point at it on the
map **before** anyone commits rather than describe it afterwards.

Put the token down when the party first sees into the room. Do not hide it, do not spring it, and
do not use it to punish. It is information, given early, so the choice is a real one.

## At the table

**It never acts.** It is not in the initiative order, it has no turn, and it will not be attacked
by anything in this house — the cultists are its family.

**It cannot be left behind.** Sera will not leave the room while the crib is in it, which means a
party that wants the children out has to carry the baby or carry Sera. One round, one free hand.

**If it dies, say so plainly and move on.** Do not linger on it, do not describe it, and do not
make a speech. The weight lands harder for being understated, and the party will supply the rest
themselves.

Carried out alive with the other three, it is part of the **800 XP** award in
[the story record](../story/the-southshore-job.md).
