## Description

> A man on top of the cairn, in a mustard-yellow coat with one sleeve torn off. He is crouched on the stones with a handaxe in one fist and a rock
> in the other, and he has been there long enough that he has built himself a little wall of
> rocks along the edge to throw. His left leg is wrapped from ankle to knee in a strip of coat,
> black with old blood. There is an iron disc on a thong at his throat, stamped with a maze. He
> has not shaved or slept in some days.
>
> When he sees you he does not shout for help. He looks at you, and then at the wolves, and then
> at you again, working out which of you is the problem.

## Tactics

**He holds the top of the cairn and throws stones.** He will not come down while a wolf is
standing, and he cannot run on that leg anyway. If a wolf climbs to him he uses the axe.

If the party loses, he stays where he is and the wolves wait. If the party wins, he sits down
on the top stone and does not get up for a while.

## Roleplaying

**He is not a believer and never was.** He came north for the Templars' pay, like the rest of
them. The maze at his throat is a meal ticket. He is from Sevenarches, and he wants to go back to
Sevenarches and never see another maze again.

**What happened.** For ten weeks he sat on a rise above the western road and counted crusaders,
and it was the easiest work he ever had. Then the counting stopped, and men started going down
the hatch. Eleven of them. The eleventh was his bunkmate.

**Two nights ago**, after dark, Garth and a lad called **Tam Ricket** went out through the north
breach and walked the markers towards Drezen, because it was the only line across the Marchlands
either of them knew. They got three hours. The wolves came up out of the dark before dawn, just
short of this cairn, and took Tam in the grass below it. They took him slowly. Garth got up the
stones, and he has been up there since: the rest of that night, all the next day, another night,
and most of today, with half a skin of water and no food.

**Where he was going.** To Drezen, to the cliff, to the ladder, and he had not worked out what he
was going to do about the ladder being heaped at the top. He was going to stand at the bottom
and shout for the Keeper. **Ask him who the Keeper is and he will tell you: the man who pays
them.** He has not been paid in three weeks. Nobody came along the markers to bring it.

### What the house did to him

**Deskari's people go mad.** Everybody in Mendev knows it, the way everybody knows the Worldwound
spreads: the Locust Host gets into the head of anyone who listens to it long enough. Garth spent a
year in a house with Deskari's acolyte, and the last of it with her praying aloud at night to
something under the floor, and the thing under the floor humming back. And on the nights he
could not sleep, **a very small voice beside his ear** told him which of them would go down the
hatch next. It was usually right. **He listened too long.** It got in.

The voice was **Nib**, amusing herself. Garth does not know that. He only knows it was real.

**Play him frightened, not funny.** Nothing here is a joke at his expense. The company should
come away from him uneasy, and a little sorry, and not quite sure how much of what he told them
was true.

- **The hum.** He hears a droning under everything, like a field of locusts a long way off. Nobody
  else hears it. It gets louder when he is afraid, which is always, and in the worst of it he
  presses the heels of his hands into his ears and waits. When the party talks to him he sometimes
  stops mid-answer and listens to something behind them.
- **Tam is still with him.** Tam sits beside him on the cairn. Garth talks to him, and waits for
  answers, and gets them. He knows Tam is dead (he watched it) and it does not stop him: *"I know.
  He knows too. He's here anyway."* Tam tells him things, and Garth passes them on.
- **He is checking your shoulders.** Something small and invisible rides on people's shoulders and
  whispers to them; he is certain of it. He will not come down off the cairn until **every one of
  the company has swept a hand over both shoulders**, slowly, where he can see, and shaken out
  their cloak. He is not joking and he will not be hurried. He slaps at his own shoulders when he
  thinks nobody is looking. **He is right about Ysolde**, and nobody will believe him.
- **Riven is the worst thing that could have walked up that rise.** A tiefling, with horns, out of
  the Worldwound dusk, three hours from a house where a demon talks in the dark. Garth will be
  sure, at least once, that Riven has come for him. It is up to Riven to change his mind, and it
  should be hard.
- **Something is under his skin.** He scratches the inside of his left forearm until it bleeds,
  and he will ask somebody, quietly, to look and tell him whether there is anything moving in
  there. **There is not.** Whoever looks has to decide what to tell him.
- **The wolves were sent.** He believes the wolves are the Locust Host's, set on him for leaving,
  and that more will come. They are only wolves. Nothing anyone says will convince him.
- **The notches.** The haft of his axe is cut end to end with small marks. The first eleven are
  the men who went down the hatch. He will not say what the rest are. If pressed: *"Every time it
  gets louder."*

### In the fight

**When the hum is loud, he is not sure who the enemy is.** At the start of each of his turns roll
a d6. **On a 1**, he throws his stone at the nearest member of the company he has not yet decided
to trust instead of at a wolf (Riven, until Riven has earned otherwise). He apologises
afterwards, every time, and means it.

### What Tam says

When the company asks Garth about the house, some of it comes from Garth and some of it from Tam,
and Garth says which: *"Tam says..."* **Garth's own answers are true. Tam's are a mix**, and
neither Garth nor the players can tell which is which. Use these, or make your own:

| Tam says | True? |
|---|---|
| *"There's two of us left in there, and her."* | **True.** |
| *"The holes in the walls are north and east. North's the quiet one."* | **True.** |
| *"She's got something on her shoulder. It talks to her. It talked to us."* | **True**, and nobody will believe it. |
| *"Don't touch the hatch. It's listening."* | Not as such. It is not a good idea either. |
| *"The hat does it. Take the hat and they stop."* | **False.** Ysolde believes it too. |
| *"She keeps the book in the oven."* | **False.** The ledger is in the store. |
| *"The ants are going to Drezen. They've been going a year."* | **True.** |
| *"Tam says I can go home now."* | Whatever you decide. |

**Cure.** Mundane rest does nothing. A ***remove curse*** or ***restoration*** would end it, and
nobody in this company can cast either. In Drezen, the Hidden Temple of Sarenrae might take him, if
somebody brought him there and asked. Left alone, he gets worse.

### The purse

If the party carries the purse sealed with the maze and marked *Y. K., the halfway house*, and
he sees it, he knows it at once. **That is his pay.** His and seventeen other men's, of whom
two are still alive. He will not ask for it. He will look at it.

**Whether they give him any of it is the scene.** Do not push it either way.

### What he knows

He knows the house well and what is under it badly. Give him all of this if they ask, and let
them ask:

- **There are two of them left, and Ysolde.** He says *two* with some care: there were three when
  he went, and he was one of them. The two live in the kitchen and do as they are told. The rest
  of the house is hers.
- **She wears a crown.** *"With feelers on it. Brass. On springs."* He does not smile when he says
  this, and he does not understand why anybody else would. *"She puts her hand on it and they
  come up."*
- **There are two holes in the walls**, north and east, and no front door.
- **There is a hatch in the floor** of her room, and the ants come up it when she calls them.
  Eleven men went down it. The first one down was **the magister**, Gell, who ran the post, a year
  ago, and nobody ever said where he went. *"After him it was hers."*
- **They are digging.** The men haul the spoil up and tip it in the ravine at night. The ants dig.
  *"East,"* he says. *"Always east."* He does not know why.
- **Ysolde stopped counting three weeks ago.** The ledger is in the store.

**What he does not know:** what the queen is, or how many soldiers there are, or what Nib is. He
has an opinion about what is under the floor, and it is nine words long: *"Whatever's down there,
it never sent anyone back up."*

### What he wants

To go south, alive, and not be hanged. A crusader patrol would hang him on sight for the maze
at his throat, and he knows it. He will take it off if he is told to, and look at it in his hand
for a while before he throws it away.

**He will not go back to the house.** Not as a guide, not for money. He will draw it in the dirt
for them, and point, and that is all.
