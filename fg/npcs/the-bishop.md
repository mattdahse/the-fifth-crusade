## Description

> The heap in the pit moves. It is the size of a cart and the colour of what it lives in: three
> thick legs, a mouth in the middle of everything, and on a long stalk above the mouth an eye that
> turns to find you and stops. A tentacle lifts out of the refuse, tipped with a club of spiked
> gristle, and wavers politely. "Is it Sunday," says the Bishop, in a voice like a drain clearing.
> "The quiet ones came on Sunday. Is Marta with you? Marta brings the peelings."

## Special abilities

- **Grab** on a tentacle hit, then **constrict 1d6+2** every round it holds on. It hauls what it
  holds into the pit.
- **Disease: filth fever**, on the bite. Fortitude **DC 14**, onset 1d3 days, 1d3 Dex and 1d3 Con
  damage a day, two consecutive saves to shake it.
- **Reach 10 ft., 15 with a tentacle.** It does not need to leave the pit to reach the ledge
  round it, and it does not leave the pit.

## Tactics

- **It does not start fights.** It attacks what attacks it, what throws something into the pit
  that is not food, and what stands in reach of it holding food and does not hand it over.
- **It fights from the pit** and never leaves it; the crawl out of the midden is five feet wide
  and it is ten.
- **It stops the moment it is fed.** A fighting otyugh given a sack of grain eats the sack of
  grain. This is not a trick. It is the thing it most wants in the world.

## Roleplaying

It was in the sink when the slaves broke into it, seventy years ago, and it ate the earth they
could not hide. They named it the Bishop because it sat in the dark and took what it was given and
never once asked anybody for anything else. It learned Common from them, a word at a time, and it
still has their words: it asks after people forty years dead, it knows it is Sunday by whether
anyone comes, and it will sometimes hum four notes of something the diggers sang, and stop, because
it has forgotten the rest.

The Labyrinth feeds it offal and, when they have them, bodies. It does not mind which. The Horn
brings the offal by hand and talks to it, and it is fond of him.

- **Attitude:** indifferent. **Diplomacy DC 13** makes it friendly. **Any food at all** does the
  same without a roll, and grain from the store counts, a sack at a time.
- **What it wants:** food, and news. *"Are the horned ones gone?"* It means the demons. Tell it
  they are, and it is quiet for a long moment. *"Then who is it that feeds me?"*
- **What it knows:** the whole Low Road, by smell. That the roof of the cavern has mouths. That
  the crawl comes out under the cavern's south end. That a boy with a torch went into the
  cavern three weeks ago and did not come out the other side.
- **Friendly, it lets the party through the midden to the crawl** and watches them go with the
  eye, all the way.
