## Description

> A thin man in his thirties in a clerk's black coat gone shiny at the elbows, ink on two fingers,
> clutching a satchel of papers to his chest. He is listening to the boy on the crate with a look
> on his face that says he has heard this speech before, somewhere worse.

## Tactics

- **He screams.** In the first round he screams *"Goblin!"* and points at Diddle, and in the second
  he screams *"Demon! Tiefling! It's one of them!"* and points at Kora. He does not stop shouting
  until someone makes him: **Intimidate DC 10** or **Diplomacy DC 12**, or a hand over his mouth.
- **He does not run toward the ends of the street.** He backs away from whichever of the party he is
  most afraid of, which puts him in the path of the centipedes, and he does not notice.

## Roleplaying

**He was a clerk to a counting-house in Kenabres**, and he saw the city opened in Arodus. Since then
every horn and tail and yellow eye is the thing that came out of the sky. He is not cruel. He is
broken in a way that makes him dangerous to anyone who looks different, and he does not know it.

**He is the blame, given a face.** The crowd picks up what he shouts. If he is shut up early, the
blame never gets going and the Preservers arrive to a street that has already decided.

**If Kora or Diddle saves his life**, he is mortified, and he does not know how to live with it. He
does not apologise in the street. He finds them later and does it properly, stiffly, formally, in
writing, and then asks if there is anything a clerk can do for them. There is: he reads and writes
four languages and he knows how a counting-house keeps its books.

- **What he wants:** never to see Kenabres happen again.
- **Bribe?** Not for anything. Offered money by a tiefling, he would faint.
