## Description

> A gangly boy of about seventeen in a Preserver's plain white tabard, a size too large, with the
> sword of Iomedae stitched badly over the heart. He has a new whetstone in his hand and no weapon
> at all. He is standing very straight and looking around as if for an officer.

## Special abilities

**He knows the field-dressing drill.** Given an order to see to someone, he gets there and makes a
**Heal check (+4)** to stabilize them, and he does it well.

## Tactics

- **He freezes, then waits.** Nobody has ever taught him what to do in a fight: he is one of Elara
  Dawnstrider's recruits, and there are no instructors. He stands where he is and looks for an
  officer, and there is none.
- **Give him an order and he obeys it instantly.** *"You! Get that woman out!"* works. So does
  *"Hold this alley."* He does whatever the first confident voice tells him, and a character who
  realises that has an extra pair of hands for the fight.
- Unarmed, he does not attack. If he is told to, he picks up a pole from a tarp frame and tries.

## Roleplaying

**He joined the Preservers the week Drezen fell** because the Preservers are Harlock Greyforge's,
and Harlock Greyforge is the greatest hero he has ever seen, from a distance. Since then he has been
drilled with a spear by other recruits who do not know how either, and he has stood guard on a lot
of doors.

- **He is a line straight to Elara's problem**: she has recruits and nobody to teach them, and here
  is one of them, in the street, not knowing what to do. Whoever gave him an order that worked has,
  in his eyes, just become his instructor, and he will ask them, very seriously, to teach him more.
- **When the Preservers arrive** he tries to explain, and they ignore him because he is a recruit.
  If one of the party vouched for him, or he was seen doing something brave, they listen.
- **What he wants:** to be good at this. Badly.
