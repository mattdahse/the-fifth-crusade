## Description

> The sacks at the back of the heap are moving. Something grey and the size of a terrier comes up
> out of a split one with grain in its whiskers, looks at you, and does not bother to run.

@link ability: filth_fever_dire_rat

## Special abilities

- **Disease: filth fever**, on the bite. Fortitude **DC 11**, onset 1d3 days, 1d3 Dex and 1d3 Con
  damage a day, two consecutive saves to shake it.

## Tactics

They fight for the grain, not for the room. A rat that is driven back from the sacks goes round
and comes back to them. Three of them together swarm whoever is standing nearest the heap.

**They can be moved without a fight.** Smoke, fire at the doorway, or a split sack dragged out
into the tunnel: they follow the grain, and anything that makes the heap less comfortable than the
dark sends them off into the old ways.

## Roleplaying

None. They are the one honest accounting in the Low Road: everything the Keeper wrote down as
*Sent West*, they have eaten.
