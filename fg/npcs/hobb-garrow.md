## Description

> A huge, red-faced man in his fifties in a stained cook's smock over what is plainly an old army
> tunic, arguing with a greengrocer over a string of onions. He has forearms like hams and a
> meat cleaver pushed through his belt, and when he shouts, people on the other side of the
> street stop and look.

None.

## Tactics

- **He charges the nearest centipede** with the cleaver on his first turn, bellowing. He hits
  harder than a centipede does and he has more hit points than any other bystander, and he is
  still going to lose if he is left alone with one, because it is harder to hit than he is.
- **He does not retreat.** He stops when the centipede is dead or he is down.
- **He takes orders.** A character who shouts one at him in a voice that sounds like a sergeant
  gets it obeyed, which is a way to put him somewhere useful.

## Roleplaying

**He was a soldier in the Fourth Crusade and has been a cook ever since**, and he cooks
for the Preservers' barracks now. He knows every one of them by name, he feeds them, and they are
a little afraid of him.

**He is the party's best defence against the blame.** When the two Preservers push up the street
and level their spears at a goblin and a tiefling, Hobb, if he is standing, bellows at them to put
those down, and they do, because he is the cook. If he fought beside any of the party, he says so,
with a great deal of profanity.

- **What he wants:** a fight worth having, one more time, and then his supper.
- **Afterwards** he wants to buy whoever fought beside him a drink, and he will not take no for an
  answer. He is a line into the Preservers' barracks, and he will talk about them to anyone who is
  eating his food.
