## Description

> A stallholder of about forty in a faded blue headscarf and a brown work dress, standing on a
> rug laid with pots, pans and ladles. She is holding a pair of brass merchant's scales against
> her chest with both hands, as though she might have to defend them, and she has not moved.

## Special abilities

None.

## Tactics

- **She freezes.** For the first two rounds she does not move at all, unless something bites her.
  From round 3 she moves like the rest of the crowd, toward the nearest way out, still holding
  the scales.
- **She is watching everything.** Whatever happens within sight of her rug, she will be able to
  say afterwards, in order.

## Roleplaying

**She is the best witness in the street and the most dangerous one.** She weighs true: it is the
thing she is proudest of in the world, the only honest scales in Lupenor's Market, and she has had
a row with every trader on the street to prove it. She talks the same way.

**When the Preservers arrive she tells them exactly what happened**, in order, in a loud flat
voice, with nothing added and nothing left out. That settles the blame in the party's favour, if
the party earned it. It also includes the bomb that splashed the herbalist, the spell that went
over a stallholder's head, and anyone who stepped over a bitten child to get at Jory. She is not
on anyone's side. She is on the side of the scales.

- **Bribe?** Never, and trying is the one sure way to make an enemy of her.
- **What she wants:** fair dealing, and to be believed.
- **Afterwards** she remembers the company by name and by what they did, and she is the person on
  this street most likely to vouch for them to a stranger, because everyone knows she will not lie.
