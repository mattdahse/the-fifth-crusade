An acolyte takes the name Hookhand on being given a warband, and wears an iron hook lashed
over the left hand from that day. It is a poor weapon and a very good argument: the hook is
what the squatters look at when they are deciding whether to run.

She opens with *burning hands* if two or more of the party are standing close together, and
spends the rest of the fight keeping her own people upright. Kill the acolyte and the
squatters will usually break.
