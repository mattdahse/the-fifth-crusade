## Description

> A very big man in scale mail cut from three different coats, and on his head a great horned helm
> made for a much bigger skull. It sits on his shoulders like a bucket. The horns clear the tunnel
> roof by a finger. Two eye-slits, set too wide for a human face, turn slowly to find you, and
> the voice that comes out of the helm arrives a moment after he speaks, because it has to come
> round the inside first.

## Special abilities

**The horned helm.** It came up the ladder three weeks ago, off a dead sergeant at the manor, with
a note addressed to *the Master of the Drezen Way*. Gerrit put it on and has not taken it off. The
eye-slits were cut for a wider face.

- **Perception -4** (already on the sheet), and he cannot see to either side. **Anyone attacking
  him from a side or rear square gets +2, as if flanking**, whether or not anybody stands
  opposite.
- **Knock it off** with a disarm or a dirty trick against **CMD 16** and he spends his next turn
  picking it up. He will not fight bareheaded; without it he is not sure who he is.
- **The echo.** He hears Baphomet in it. It is his own breathing.

## Tactics

- **He holds the Gate** and nothing else: the one-square gap in the crate barricade, with the
  greataxe. Everybody who wants the Keep comes to him, one at a time.
- **He goes for the biggest one first**, because that is the fair fight, and says so.
- **He does not pursue** past the barricade. He was told to hold it, and he was told that by
  himself, which settles it.
- **He yields at 8 hit points or fewer.** He kneels, takes the helm off with both hands and asks
  to be killed properly or not at all. He means either.
- **Single combat.** Challenge him, champion against champion, to a yield, and he accepts at
  once and keeps the terms. Nobody else on his side interferes, because nobody else on his side
  is at the Gate.

## Roleplaying

A quarryman from the Marchlands who could lift what two men could, and whom the Labyrinth fed when
nobody else would. He is brave, slow, sincere, and **cannot lie**: ask him where the Keeper is and
he will tell you, and what the Keeper is planning, as far as he knows it, which is not far.

**He believes he is the Master of the Drezen Way** because the helm came to him. The note said
*Send men.* He wants to be the man sent: to lead the relief detail west and bring the manor's
people home. For three weeks the Keeper has countermanded every order he has given, and he is
ashamed that nobody has gone.

He feeds the Bishop by hand and talks to it. It is the only creature in the Low Road that is glad
to see him.

- **Can he be bought?** No. He can be **persuaded**, which is different, and easier.
- **Three ways past him without a fight.** Show him the Keeper's packed bag, or tell him about it
  and let him look: he goes to arrest the clerk himself, and the party can walk behind him. Call
  him *Master* and mean it: he lets them through to "deal with the clerk." Or give him his fight,
  one against one, to a yield.
- **What he wants:** to be sent. A party that lets him lead the relief west is sending him to the
  manor, where Ysolde Karn will be delighted to see a big man who does what he is told.
- **Will he turn on the people he works for?** On the Keeper, gladly, the moment he has proof.
  On the order, never. He has not met it, and it sent him a hat.
