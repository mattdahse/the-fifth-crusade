## Description

> A tiny old woman in a Sarkorian shawl of faded red wool, sitting on an upturned bucket by the
> brazier with her hands held out to the coals. Her face is creased as a walnut. She has a crust
> in her lap she has not eaten, and she watches everything with enormous, patient eyes.

## Special abilities

None.

## Tactics

- **She does not move.** When the lid comes off she stays on her bucket, hands to the fire, and
  watches. A centipede that reaches her bites her, and she lets it.
- **Two hit points.** If she is left where she is, she is very likely to die, and she knows it, and
  she does not seem to mind. That is what should frighten the party.

## Roleplaying

**She was a slave in Drezen for fifty years**, carried off from a Sarkorian steading as a girl and
kept by the demons who held this city until the 13th of Neth. She has been free for a few weeks.
She speaks Abyssal, because she had to, and she knows the streets and the underways of Drezen
better than anyone living, because she was made to dig some of them.

**She settles the question of the tiefling in a sentence.** When somebody points at Kora and screams
*demon*, Old Hrenna, from her bucket, in a voice that carries the length of the street, says:
*"That one's no demon. I know what demons look like."* The crowd goes quiet. Use it if the blame
starts going badly; it is a better answer than a Diplomacy check.

- **What she wants:** nothing, now. She is warming her hands. That is the whole of it.
- **Afterwards** she talks to whoever saved her, if anyone did, and she will tell them things about
  this city's cellars and tunnels that nobody in the crusade knows. She asks nothing for it. She
  just wants somebody to know.
- Treat her with care at the table. She is not a joke and not a victim; she is a survivor, and she
  is the calmest person in the market.
