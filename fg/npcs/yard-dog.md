## Description

> Big, short-haired, scarred about the muzzle, and far too quiet. It does not bark at you. It
> comes off the chain at a dead run with its head low, and the second one is already coming round
> the other side of the woodpile.

## Special abilities

**Trip** on the bite. A hit lets it make a free trip attempt at CMB +3 without provoking, and a
prone character in a scrapyard full of edges is in real trouble. That is the whole danger here.

## Tactics

They are trained and they work as a pair — one goes for the front rank and the other goes wide
for whoever is at the back. **Speed 40** means neither can be outrun.

They fight until dropped, and they can be dropped non-lethally by anyone who thinks to. A party
that kills two dogs loudly at the front gate has told the entire house it is coming.

## Roleplaying

**They can be handled.** Meat quiets them; a Handle Animal or Wild Empathy check buys silence
rather than affection. They are not evil and they are not cultists, and a party that works out
the difference will be glad of it later, because the dogs are the one thing in this yard that
can be dealt with kindly and completely.
