## Description

> A woman in her thirties, heavily pregnant, in a scrapper's apron over a house dress. She has a
> carving knife in her right hand and her left arm out flat across the doorway behind her, and
> she is not shouting. She has put herself exactly where she means to stay.

## Special abilities

None, and the numbers are deliberately poor. **She is not a fight.** She is a decision the party
has to make while people are shouting, and the statblock exists so there is something to roll if
they insist — not because it is meant to be rolled.

## Tactics

- **She does not advance. Ever.** She holds one doorway and attacks only what comes within reach
  of it.
- **She is trivially easy to put down** — 6 hit points, AC 11 — and that is the point. Anyone who
  swings at her drops her, and then has to look at what they did.
- **Non-lethal damage, a grapple, a disarm, or simply stepping past her** all work, and a party
  that thinks of any of them has solved the room.

## Roleplaying

She wears the maze because Hesk does, and she stopped pretending to herself some time ago that the
men in the cellar are lodgers. She is not a fanatic. She is a woman who has decided that whatever
happens next happens to her first.

**She surrenders the moment the children are safe, and not one second before.** Say the children
will not be touched, and mean it, and the knife goes down. Say anything else and she does not
hear it.

**She knows what is under the house** — the door, the tunnel, the rope, the map, all of it — and
she will trade every word of it for the children. She is the fastest route to the intel in the
building and she is standing in the open.

## The children

**Sera is twelve. Corin is nine.** They are in the room behind her, and the baby is in a crib in
the corner with them. They are **not NPCs, they have no statblocks, and they are never in the
initiative order.** They hide, and they run, and if a fight happens in that room they get hurt by
accident — which the GM should let land rather than engineer.

Taking Alia and all three children out alive is worth **800 XP**, and the story record says so
out loud. Mira's people take them either way; the cell is broken either way. The only difference
is whether the party chose to make it.
