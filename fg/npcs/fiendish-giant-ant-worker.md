## Description

> The galleries are full of them. Pale dun shells the size of a dog, going about in both
> directions at once, antennae ticking over the walls, carrying things: a grub, a stone, a
> mouthful of earth, part of a boot. They part around you the way water parts around a post and
> close up again behind. None of them look at you. Somewhere ahead of you one of them is very
> carefully carrying away something that you dropped.

## Special abilities

- **Non-combatant.** Workers **do not attack** unless they are attacked, cornered, or something
  threatens the eggs in front of them. Then they bite once, and go back to work. **They are worth
  no XP**, and the company should never be made to clear them. They are the scenery, and the
  scenery is moving.
- **The crowd.** Any square in a gallery with workers in it is **difficult terrain**. A character
  can push straight through a worker's square with a **DC 10 Acrobatics or Strength check** as part
  of moving; failing ends that move. Workers never block the soldiers or the queen.
- **Tidy.** Workers carry anything that does not belong in the warren out to the larder: dropped
  weapons, lit torches, the dead, and **charges**. At the end of each round, any loose object in
  a square next to a worker has a **50% chance** of being picked up and carried 20 feet toward the
  larder. A charge that has been **wedged** (see **Bringing Down the Roof**) cannot be lifted.
- **Resist cold 5, fire 5**, and mindless, like the soldiers.

## Tactics

They do not have any. They work. When the roof starts to come down they keep working, and that
is the last thing anybody sees of most of them.

## Roleplaying

None. But the company will try, and it is worth noticing out loud that the workers are doing
exactly what the men upstairs were doing: hauling spoil, shoring the passages, and feeding the
thing in the big chamber. Nobody asked them either.
