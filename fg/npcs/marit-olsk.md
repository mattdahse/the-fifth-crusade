## Tactics

- **She does not run. She shouts at Jory.** On the first round, and every round he is still in
  sight, she calls him by name: *"Jory! Jory Tallow, get down from there, what would your master
  say?"* That is how the party learns his name mid-fight, if they learn it at all.
- If a centipede comes for her she climbs down off the crate and makes for the nearest alley like
  anyone else, still shouting.

## Roleplaying

**She is from Kenabres, and she knew Jory there.** She bought her candles at his master's shop every
week for years and watched him grow up behind the counter, a skinny boy who dripped wax on
everything. She has not seen him since the city fell, and she thought he was dead.

**She speaks for Jory afterwards.** When the party and Mira come to decide what to do with him, she
is the one who finds them first, to ask. She does not excuse him. She says what he was before
Arodus and what he saw, and that his master burned. Then she says she will take him, if someone
lets her: she has a room, and needles, and she can teach a boy to sew.

That is a fourth answer to the question of Jory. Mira will think it is lovely.

- **What she wants:** one thing from Kenabres to come out of all this whole.
- **Bribe?** She has nothing. She would sew for them, which is not nothing, and she will say so.
