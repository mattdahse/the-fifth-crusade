## Description

> A red-handed young woman with a bundle of washing under one arm and a fat two-year-old boy on her
> other hip, gnawing a crust. She has the look of someone who has been awake since before dawn
> for as long as she can remember.

## Special abilities

None.

## Tactics

- **She drags the child under the nearest cart** and lies on top of him. She does not move again
  until a centipede comes within reach, and then she does not move either: she takes the bite.
- **The boy, Benno, is under her.** He is not a separate token. As long as she is conscious, nothing
  reaches him. If she drops, the next bite does.
- She screams for help, constantly, at the top of her voice. The whole table hears where she is.

## Roleplaying

**Her husband was a crusader who did not come back from the taking**, and she has not had time to
grieve because there is washing. She launders for the officers' quarters on the hill, which makes
her invisible in the way servants are invisible: she goes into every officer's house in Drezen once
a week and nobody looks at her.

- **If the party helps her,** she tells the wash-yards, and the wash-yards tell everyone. By the
  end of the week every laundress and scullion in Drezen knows the company's faces and what they
  did. Mira, told this, will nearly faint with joy.
- **What she wants:** her son to grow up.
- **Bribe?** She has no money and wants none. A favour done for her is repaid with gossip, which is
  worth more.
