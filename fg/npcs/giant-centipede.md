## Description

> Something as long as a man is tall comes out of the overturned crate in one continuous pour of
> legs, red-brown and glossy, and goes straight up the pole of the nearest tarp. Another follows
> it, and another. The sound they make on the canvas overhead is like rain.

## Special abilities

- **Poison** on the bite. Fort DC 13, 1d3 Dexterity per round for 6 rounds, cured by one save.
- **Climb 40 ft.** Tarps, cart covers, lean-tos, stacked crates and the house fronts along the
  street are all floor to a centipede. It can come at a character from above, and it can get away from one the same way.
- **Mindless.** Immune to every mind-affecting effect. It cannot be frightened, calmed, charmed
  or reasoned with, and *sleep* does nothing to it.

## Tactics

**Five hit points.** Any solid hit kills one, and a bomb's splash can kill two. The danger is not
any single centipede; it is four of them loose at once in a crowd, going in four directions.

**They scatter.** Out of the crate, each one goes for a different nearest living thing, and in
this market that is usually a bystander rather than an adventurer. They do not hunt as a group
and they do not protect Jory.

**They use the height.** A centipede with nothing to bite climbs, and the next round it drops off
a tarp or an eave onto whoever is underneath. That is a charge from above and the target does not see it
coming unless somebody is watching the canvas.

**No morale.** They fight until they are dead. A centipede that is badly hurt and has a house
front behind it may go up it and over the roof, which leaves it loose in Drezen. The story record
says what that costs.

## Roleplaying

None. They are vermin out of the Southshore cellars, fed on rats for three weeks in a crate, and
the only unnatural thing about them is the person who thought a demon lord would be proud of them.
