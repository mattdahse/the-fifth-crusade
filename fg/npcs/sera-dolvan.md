## Description

> A girl of twelve in a scrapper's smock two sizes too big, with her brother's wrist in one hand
> and her back against the crib. She has already worked out that the door is behind the shouting
> and she is not going that way.

## Why she has a statblock

**So she is on the map, in a square, before anybody aims.** She has four hit points and no attack.
The numbers are not here to be rolled against — they are here so that a *burning hands* cone, a
badly placed *colour spray*, or a summoned animal told to go through that door has an obvious and
visible cost that the party can see coming rather than discover afterwards.

## Tactics

**She never attacks and never rolls initiative for her own sake — but she does move**, and where
she moves is the problem.

- **She goes to the crib**, always. Every round she is not restrained, she moves toward the baby
  and puts herself between it and whatever is loudest.
- **She drags Corin with her**, so the two of them are usually in adjacent squares and usually in
  the worst one.
- **She does not flee the room** while the baby is in it, which means "wait for them to run" is
  not a plan that works.
- **She can be grabbed, carried, shoved into a corner, or talked to.** All of those work. A
  character who spends one round physically moving the children has solved the whole problem, and
  that round is the price of doing it properly.

## Roleplaying

She knows what the cellar is. She has known for about a year, and she has not told her brother.
She is not a cultist and she is not innocent of it either — she has carried food down those stairs.

Taken alive she will not speak in front of her mother, and will say a great deal once separated:
the tunnel, the men in it, the rope, and the fact that her father has been frightened since
midwinter.
