## Description

> A girl of about seven with a flour-dusted plait, curled up in the dark under the baker's
> handcart with her knees to her chin. She is not crying. She is watching whatever is coming,
> very hard.

None.

## Tactics

- **She does not move.** Pip is under the handcart and stays there, whatever happens, until
  someone she trusts comes for her. That makes her the easiest target in the street for a
  centipede that goes under the cart, and the hardest person in the street to reach, because
  getting to her means **crawling**: prone, into a space a Medium creature has to squeeze into.
- **The cart gives her cover** from anything thrown from the street. It does not keep out the
  splash of a bomb that lands beside it. Say so before Diddle throws.
- **Two hit points.** One bite drops her.

## Roleplaying

**She comes out for exactly two people:** her father, and whoever is nearest her own height. That
is Diddle. A goblin crouching at the cart's edge and holding out a hand is, to Pip, the least
frightening thing in the street, and she takes the hand. Nobody at the table will expect that,
and it is the best moment the scene has to offer, so let it happen if it can.

Afterwards she wants to know the goblin's name, and she remembers it. Next time the company comes
through the market she has drawn him in flour on the cart, with the ears much too large.
