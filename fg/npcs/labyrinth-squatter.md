They are not fanatics and they are not soldiers. Most of them came north for pay and stayed
because there was nowhere to go back to, and the Labyrinth feeds them. A squatter fights
hard while he is winning and negotiates the moment he is not — which is worth knowing, and
worth exploiting.

Each carries a hooked iron token stamped with the Labyrinth's maze. They are told it marks
them as claimed, and that losing it is worse than dying. Most of them believe it.
