## Where the numbers come from

The base is the SRD **Ant, Giant**, read out of `PF-SRD-Bestiary.mod` rather than from memory —
that record is the CR 2 soldier, so it is cut down to the Bestiary's **drone**: the sting, the
poison and the bite's grab all go, Con drops from 17 to 14, and the CR with them. What is left is
a 2 HD vermin with one bite, and that bite takes one-and-a-half times Strength because it is the
creature's only natural attack, which is where `1d6+3` comes from on a Strength of 14.

Then the **fiendish** simple template, which at 2 HD is cheap: darkvision it already had,
resistance 5 to cold and fire, an evil alignment, and smite good once a day. No damage reduction
and no spell resistance — those start at 5 HD — and no CR increase at 4 HD or under, so this
stays CR 1.

**Worth checking before the session if it matters to you:** the fiendish template is the one part
here not taken from a module on this machine, because none of the installed bestiaries carry it.
The rest is derived from a record that is.

**Intelligence 0 means no Intelligence score at all** — the dash on a vermin statblock. It is
mindless, immune to anything mind-affecting, and it cannot be talked to, frightened, or bribed.

## At the table

They do not fight like soldiers, because they are not soldiers. A drone digs, hauls, and tends
the clutch, and it defends the nest the way a worker defends a nest: immediately, without
assessing, and without ever breaking off. There is no morale check coming. It fights until it is
dead, and so does the next one.

The fiendish part is not a costume. Something in the Worldwound got into this hive some
generations back, and what came out the other side burns cold to the touch and does not belong on
this plane any more. It is still an ant. That is the unsettling part.

Speed **50 feet** is the number that matters and the one that surprises players. Nothing at this
level outruns a drone in a straight line, and the tunnel is not a straight line — anyone who
decides to fall back down it is choosing to be caught somewhere worse.
