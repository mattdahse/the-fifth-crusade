## Description

> It is the size of a pony and it does not care that you are there in any way you can use. The
> carapace is the dark red-brown of dried blood, dull as horn, scratched down the flanks from
> years of squeezing through its own tunnels. The mandibles are serrated and they are already
> open. Its eyes catch a cold amber light, and there is nothing in this hole that is casting it.

## Special abilities

- **Smite good 1/day** — swift action, +0 to hit and +2 damage against a good target. Linked
  above; the drones will spend it on whoever is most obviously righteous.
- **Resist cold 5, fire 5** — from the fiendish template, and the reason a 1st-level party's
  usual answers land soft.
- **Mindless.** Intelligence is not low, it is absent. Immune to every mind-affecting effect,
  and it cannot be frightened, charmed, bluffed, intimidated or reasoned with.
- No poison and no grab. Those belong to the soldier, not the drone.

## Tactics

Straight at the nearest warm body by the shortest line, and bite it until one of them stops.
That is the whole plan and there is not a second one.

**Speed 50 feet** is the stat that decides fights here. It moves two squares for the party's one
in a corridor built at its own width, so withdrawing down the tunnel is not a retreat, it is
choosing to be caught somewhere narrower. A drone that loses its target picks the next nearest
rather than pursuing the first.

**It will not leave the clutch.** Drones fighting within reach of the egg heaps hold that ground
and do not follow the party back up the tunnel — which is an exit, for a party that notices.

**No morale, ever.** Nothing here checks, flees, surrenders or is discouraged by the deaths of
the other three.

## Roleplaying

There is no roleplaying. That is the point of it, and it is worth making the table feel the
difference: **nothing about this creature can be bribed, threatened, parleyed with, or turned.**
A party that has spent the campaign so far handling people — buying off squatters, leaning on an
acolyte, reading who wants an excuse to quit — arrives here and finds every one of those tools
dead in their hands.

The fiendish part is not a personality either. Something out of the Worldwound got into this hive
some generations back and what came out burns cold and no longer entirely belongs on this plane.
It still digs. It still tends the clutch. It is still an ant, and that is the unsettling part
rather than a mercy.

## Where the numbers come from


The base is the SRD **Ant, Giant**, read out of `PF-SRD-Bestiary.mod` rather than from memory —
that record is the CR 2 soldier, so it is cut down to the Bestiary's **drone**: the sting, the
poison and the bite's grab all go, Con drops from 17 to 14, and the CR with them. What is left is
a 2 HD vermin with one bite, and that bite takes one-and-a-half times Strength because it is the
creature's only natural attack, which is where `1d6+3` comes from on a Strength of 14.

Then the **fiendish** simple template, which at 2 HD is cheap: darkvision it already had,
resistance 5 to cold and fire, an evil alignment, and smite good once a day. No damage reduction
and no spell resistance — those start at 5 HD — and no CR increase at 4 HD or under, so this
stays CR 1.

**Worth checking before the session if it matters to you:** the fiendish template is the one part
here not taken from a module on this machine, because none of the installed bestiaries carry it.
The rest is derived from a record that is.

**Intelligence 0 means no Intelligence score at all** — the dash on a vermin statblock. It is
mindless, immune to anything mind-affecting, and it cannot be talked to, frightened, or bribed.
