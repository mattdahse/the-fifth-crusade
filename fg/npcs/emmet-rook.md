## Description

> An old man on his knees in the rubble with a pick across his thighs, working a stone loose the
> way you would unpick a knot: patiently, and without looking at it. A lantern by his knee is
> turned down so far it is barely lit. He does not turn round. "If you are the Keeper's," he says,
> "tell him it is two yards, not three, and the third one is not coming."

## Special abilities

**He knows the Low Road in the dark.** He needs no light to find his way anywhere in it, and a
party he is guiding moves through the old ways at full speed and **never meets the centipedes**
unless it wants to.

## Tactics

He does not fight. If somebody swings at him he puts his arms over his head and waits for it to
be over, which is a thing he learned a long time ago and has not needed to unlearn.

## Roleplaying

He came to Drezen as a boy of nine, from a border village the demons emptied, and dug the Low Road
for forty-one years with a spoon, a hoe blade and then a proper pick somebody stole for him. He
dug the Well House branch himself. He was in the Bakehouse branch the day it came down, and he got
out, and the six behind him did not. He knows their names. He is the only one left who does.

**He never went down the ladder.** "Somebody had to stay and send the ring back up." The slaves
had one ring that let a body fall slowly: the runaway wore it over the edge and they hauled it back
up the cliff on a cord for the next one. The last of them took it down the night the city was
retaken, and it did not come back up. He does not know what that means and has decided not to.
**If the party has the plain iron ring with the nick out of one edge** from Hesk Dolvan's
strongbox, show it to him. He knows it at once.

**He sold the Low Road to the Labyrinth** in Lamashan, when the crusade's bread stopped and theirs
did not. They pay him a loaf a yard to clear the old ways toward the houses. He is ashamed of it
in the way a man is ashamed of a thing he would do again.

- **How he turns:** ask him his name. Nobody down here has. They call him the Mole, and he answers
  to it the way he answered to worse. Asked his name, he gives it, and after that a **Diplomacy DC
  10** buys everything he knows. Showing him the ring does the same without a roll.
- **What he knows:** every branch, where the three houses come up, the long way round the nest,
  that the midden's crawl comes out under the cavern, that the roof of the cavern has mouths, and
  what the slaves did about it: **"You send a sack across first."** He knows what the swallows mean.
- **What he wants:** the six in the Bakehouse branch carried out. He has been clearing toward them
  for a month on the Labyrinth's bread, and the Labyrinth thinks he is clearing toward a house.
- **Will he turn on the people he works for?** He never worked for them. He sold them something.
