## Description

> A grey wolf, rangy and long in the leg, with a coat gone the colour of old ash and patched
> bare along the spine where something has eaten into the skin and stopped. Its eyes hold a dull
> red light, like a coal you have been told not to touch. It is not in a hurry. It sits down at
> the edge of throwing range, out in the open where you can see it, and yawns.

## Special abilities

- **Trip** on the bite. A hit gives a free trip attempt (CMB +2) with no attack of opportunity.
  A tripped character is prone: &#8211;4 to melee attacks, +4 to be hit in melee, and standing
  up provokes.
- **Smite good**, once a day, as a swift action, against one good-aligned creature: +2 damage on
  every bite against that target until it is dead.
- **Resist cold 5, fire 5.** Diddle's bombs lose 5 on the direct hit and the splash does nothing.
  He should find this out the hard way, once, and then be told what he is looking at.
- **Spell resistance 6.** Riven and Kora roll d20 + caster level and need a 6. They will usually
  make it, so let the first failure be a surprise.

## Tactics

**They hunt like wolves, and they enjoy it more than wolves do.** See **The Waiting Cairn** for the
whole fight. In short:

- **They pick the smallest thing.** Diddle first, then Kora. Two wolves go in on one target to
  flank it and trip it, and the third waits.
- **They fall back from the biggest thing.** A wolf facing Dorogh alone gives ground and lets
  him follow it out of the line.
- **They go when it stops being fun.** If two are down, the third runs and does not come back.
  It follows at a distance all the next day, and it is there on the rise behind them when they
  reach the valley.

## Roleplaying

Animals, mostly. The Worldwound has been in them for two generations and it shows in one way only:
**they play with what they catch.** A dying sheep in the Marchlands takes a very long time to die.
The narration may notice this. It should never admire it.
