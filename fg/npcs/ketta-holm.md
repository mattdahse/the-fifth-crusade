## Description

> A wiry woman of about thirty-five, sleeves rolled, a pencil behind one ear and her dark hair
> tied up in a rag. Bunches of dried herbs hang from the frame of her yellow tarp, and there are
> little labelled jars on a board at her elbow. She is haggling with someone and winning, and she
> sounds as though she enjoys it.

## Special abilities

**She is the one bystander who tends the wounded.** Once the nearest centipede is at least two
squares from her, she moves to the nearest person who is down and makes a **Heal check (+6)** to
stabilize them. She uses her own stock to do it, and afterwards she remembers that too.

## Tactics

- **Round 1 she saves her jars**, sweeping the board into her apron, because that is a month's
  living.
- **From round 2 she works the wounded**, as above, going to whoever is nearest and down. She
  gets out of any centipede's way, but she does not leave the market.
- **If her stall is splashed by a bomb**, she stops helping and starts shouting at the goblin.

## Roleplaying

**She followed the crusade up from Nerosyan as a camp apothecary** and stayed in Drezen because the
army pays for bandages on time. She is not warm and she is not unkind: she is busy, she is
practical, and she thinks most people are fools until shown otherwise.

**She has sold to a goblin once before**, a courier who paid in exact coin and did not haggle, and
she still does not know what to make of it. Diddle haggling over reagents is a second goblin, and
a worse haggler, and she was about to charge him double for the smell when Jory started shouting.

- **If Diddle's bombs spare her stall** and she watches him fight, she sells to him at the fair
  price for the rest of the campaign, without flinching, and complains about him to everyone
  else in a tone that makes it clear she likes him.
- **If her stall is splashed**, she refuses to sell to him at all until someone makes it right,
  with coin or a well-made apology. She can be won back. She wants to be asked properly.
- **What she knows:** reagents. She can get almost anything an alchemist of this level needs, and
  she knows who in Drezen is buying the things nobody should be.
