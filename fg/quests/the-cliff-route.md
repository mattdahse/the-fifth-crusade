# The Cliff Route

<!-- id: the_cliff_route -->
<!-- level: 1 -->
<!-- giver: Mira Thistledance -->
<!-- xp: 400 -->

**Find out where that tunnel actually comes out, and be able to draw it.**

This is the piece of intel Mira did not know she was buying. She thinks she is paying for a cell.
What the cell was *for* is a way in and out of Drezen that never passes a gate.

## Terms

- Beyond the cavern the passage narrows and opens in a **cave in the cliff face**, with a rope
  ladder spiked to the stone and heaped ready to be dropped over the lip.
- Lowered, it puts anyone on the ground outside Drezen's wall: no gate, no toll, no watch officer
  writing a name in a book. Heaped, it shows nothing from below, which is why nobody has ever
  spotted the route from outside.

## Completion

The party reaches the cave mouth and can say where the foot of the cliff comes out - **Survival
DC 15**, or the marked map, or simply dropping the ladder and climbing down to look.

A party that turns back at the cavern does not earn this, and should be told afterwards that
there was something past it.
