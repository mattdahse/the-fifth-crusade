**Look at the place before kicking the gate.**

Mira's watcher can see a gate and a yard and nothing else, so the party genuinely does not have an
order of battle. Finding out is the first real decision they make and it is worth paying for.

## Terms

- Cinder Row is a **public street** and the yard can be watched from it without a check.
- An hour of looking buys the dogs, the shift, and the fact that far more people go into that
  house at dusk than come out of it in the morning.

## Completion

The party spends time on the street before going in. **Award it afterwards rather than offering
it beforehand** - a scouting bonus announced in advance is an instruction, and this campaign is
trying to find out what they do without one.
