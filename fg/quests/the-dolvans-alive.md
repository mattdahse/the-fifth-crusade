# The Dolvans Taken Alive

<!-- id: the_dolvans_alive -->
<!-- level: 1 -->
<!-- giver: Mira Thistledance -->
<!-- xp: 800 -->

**Alia Dolvan and all three of her children walk out of that house breathing.**

Mira asked for the cell broken. She did not ask for this and she will not raise it beforehand,
because a party told in advance that mercy is worth points is not making a choice, it is
following an instruction.

## Terms

- **Alia**, seven months pregnant, standing in the doorway of the children's room with a carving
  knife and no intention of moving. She surrenders the instant the children are safe.
- **Sera, twelve. Corin, nine. An infant in a crib.** None of them attacks, none is worth combat
  XP, and all three are on the map in squares.
- Mira's people take prisoners off the party's hands at the end of Cinder Row and ask no
  questions in front of them.

## Completion

All four alive and in Mira's custody, or released - either counts. The cell is broken exactly as
thoroughly whichever way this goes, and that is the point of the award: **the only thing that
changes is what the party did.**

**This is the largest single award in the adventure and it is deliberate.** Award it even if
nobody asks, and say what it was for.
