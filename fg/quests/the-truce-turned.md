**Get the two factions to fight each other.**

Corwin Skell is waiting for something to happen to Ysolde Karn. That is his entire strategy. A
party that works out what he wants and hands it to him fights three people instead of six, on
ground of its own choosing, and can be walking into the sealed room while the rest of it happens.

## Terms

- **Corwin will deal**, and means every word of it - right up until the Labyrinth is dead and the
  party is standing between him and the sealed room.
- **Ysolde will not**, because she believes the hive is hers and Corwin is a thief.
- Neither of them is stupid, and neither will move first while the truce holds. Something has to
  break it, and the party is the only thing in the valley that can.

## Completion

The two factions come to blows, and the party is not in the middle when they do.

**Award this for the manoeuvre, not for the body count.** A party that walks in swinging fights
six at once and probably loses; a party that talks fights three, then three. Both are good
sessions. Only one of them is this award, and it is worth the difference.
