**Find out where the tunnel is going, and get word to Drezen.**

Nobody sent the company to look for this. Elara asked for a waystation and Mira asked for anything
with writing on it. What is under the halfway house is a hive digging a road east toward Drezen's
walls, a quarter of a mile so far and further every season, and nobody in the city knows.

## Terms

- **The tunnel** leaves the nest by what looks like one of the side burrows that stop. It does not
  stop. Behind a curtain of loose earth it runs on east, wide enough for a soldier, shored in places
  with the post's own timber. At its head, a quarter of a mile on, there may be soldiers digging.
- **The back of the ledger** says the same thing in figures: *The Road*, a year of feet dug, and
  *Drezen, 22 miles less 1,240 ft.*
- **Ysolde will tell them herself**, proudly, if anybody asks her what the road is for. So will
  Nib, out of spite, once Ysolde is beyond helping her. So will the two men, who dig it.

## Completion

The company can tell **Elara Dawnstrider** what the tunnel is and which way it is going: either
by walking it to its head, or by bringing back the ledger and reading its back pages, or by
bringing back somebody who digs it. **Award it for any of those.**

**Burning the clutch is not this award**, though it is what stops the digging. This one is for
knowing. A company that burns the eggs and never finds out what the ants were for has done the
right thing for the wrong reasons, and should be told afterwards how close it was.
