**Bring the roof of the queen's chamber down on the queen.**

Nobody asked for this, and it is the thing that ends the hive. The chamber lies under the dry
watercourse; two timber props are holding its roof up; Dorogh can read a roof and Diddle can make a
charge. Everything else is nerve.

## Terms

- **Knowledge (engineering)** finds the props; **Craft (alchemy)** makes the charges; somebody has
  to set them, wedge them so the workers cannot tidy them away, light them, and get out.
- The full sequence is in **Bringing Down the Roof**.

## Completion

The roof comes down on the queen, whole or in half. **Award it for the collapse, not the kill**:
the queen's own XP is separate and awarded however she dies. A company that kills her in a straight
fight and never thinks of the roof has done a hard thing the hard way; tell them afterwards what the
props were for.
