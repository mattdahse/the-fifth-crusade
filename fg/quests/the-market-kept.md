**Nobody who came to the market to shop leaves it dead.**

Nobody asked this of them. It is what Mira was watching for.

## Terms

- There is no fee and no promise. Four strangers who happened to be standing in the market did
  something about it, and Mira noticed which of them looked at the crowd before looking at the
  centipedes.

## Completion

**Every bystander on the map is alive when the last centipede dies**, all twelve, stallholders
and shoppers and Pip under the cart,, whether or not they were bitten or
went down along the way. A bystander brought back from dying by a *stabilize*, a cure or a Heal
check counts as alive, and that is the point: the award is for the people who were helped, not
for a fight nobody was hurt in.

A centipede that escaped over the roofs does **not** cost this award. It is a different problem,
and the story record covers it.
