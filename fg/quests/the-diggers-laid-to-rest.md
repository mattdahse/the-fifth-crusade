**The six who died when the Bakehouse branch came down get the rest of their escape.**

The Diggers are a haunt, and this record carries the haunt's experience: it is paid for surviving
or ending it, the way a creature's is, and more fully for ending it properly.

## Terms

- The haunt in the fall (C7) replays the collapse at anyone who enters the chamber.
- It is **destroyed** by carrying the bones out of the rubble to the ledge (C15) and letting them go
  over into the Marchlands, which is where the six were going. Emmet Rook knows this without being
  told. It is **neutralised** for a day by positive energy: a channel, or a cure spell spent on the
  place rather than a person.

## Completion

Award it when the haunt has been survived. If the bones went over the ledge, the haunt does not
reset, and Emmet Rook says their names, once each, as they go.
