**Emmet Rook walks out of the Low Road alive, and not in the Labyrinth's pay.**

He is an old man clearing rubble for bread, and the Labyrinth calls him the Mole because it never
asked his name. He is also the last person alive who dug these tunnels, and the only one who knows
the names of the six in the Bakehouse branch.

## Terms

- Nobody asks this of the party. He does not ask it either.
- He can be turned by asking his real name, by showing him the slaves' ring from Hesk Dolvan's
  strongbox, or by a Diplomacy DC 10 once he has been treated as a person.

## Completion

He leaves with the party, or is left free with the Labyrinth broken behind him, and is not handed
to anyone who would hang him for selling the tunnels. Mira will want to meet him. So, if anyone
thinks to ask her, will Old Hrenna.
