**Get the stolen grain back to the market it was stolen from.**

Nobody asked for this. Mira thinks she is paying for a cell. What is under the South Bank is also
two months of Lupenor's Market walking out of its own warehouses by night and down a hole, which
is why the price of grain on the board by the water pump has been rubbed out and written in again
three times this month.

## Terms

- The sacks in the grain store and the packing room carry the stencilled mark of Lupenor's Market.
  They are heavy: a hundredweight a sack, forty sacks, and the porters in the packing room will
  carry them for the same wage the Keeper paid.
- Mira pays a finder's fee on whatever reaches the market: a tenth of its value, about 60 gp for
  the lot, and a very long pause while she works out whose warehouse it was.

## Completion

The grain, or most of it, goes back up through the scrapyard and across the Ahari to the market,
or the party tells Mira exactly where it is and she sends for it. The price on the board comes down
within the week. **Say so at the table.**
