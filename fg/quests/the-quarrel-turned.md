# The Quarrel Turned

<!-- id: the_quarrel_turned -->
<!-- level: 1 -->
<!-- giver: Mira Thistledance -->
<!-- xp: 300 -->

**End the Keep through the Labyrinth's own quarrel rather than through a general fight.**

Two men claim to be the Master of the Drezen Way: Anselm Vauk, who holds the ledger, and Gerrit
Bask, who wears the helm. They have not spoken in three weeks. Everyone else down here is waiting
to be told which of them to obey.

## Terms

Any of these counts:

- **The Horn turned on the Keeper**, by showing him Anselm's packed bag or telling him about it.
- **The Keeper selling the Horn**, the route and the rest for a door out and his ledger.
- **Single combat**: the Horn challenged, champion against champion, to a yield, and the terms kept.
- **One of them named Master by the party** and the other given up by his own side.

## Completion

The Gate and the counting room are the party's without the two of them standing together against
it. Award it at the moment the second one stops fighting, and say what it was for.
