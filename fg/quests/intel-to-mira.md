**Get what is under that yard back to Lupenor's Market.** Mira is paying for what the party
brings back, not for a body count, and she says so before they leave.

## Terms

- **150 gp** on delivery of anything worth having, and Mira decides what is worth having.
- The piece that matters is a **rough map** carried by one of the Labyrinth initiates in the
  tunnel: the Marchlands north and west of Drezen, drawn by somebody who had walked it, with a
  line of small marks over the top that are not places.

## Completion

The map, the ledger, or the letters reach Mira's hands. **Getting it back is the reason there is
a second commission** - the marks end at a Sarkorian ruin two days along the western road, and
that is where she sends them next.
