# Hold the Halfway House

The road from Drezen to the Sarian Fane is two days on foot and there is nothing on it. Every
courier who walks it sleeps one night in the open, in country that has ash storms, abyssal
vermin, and worse.

**Elara Dawnstrider** wants that changed and has nobody to send. Iomedae's Preservers are
taking recruits faster than anyone in Drezen can train them, every sworn blade she has is
needed inside the walls, and the letter she sent to the Church of Iomedae asking for
instructors has not been answered. So she is hiring.

Halfway along the road, on the lip of the valley, stands a Sarkorian house that outlasted
its village — three walls and most of a slate roof. The company that came through in
Lamashan sheltered in it and moved on. Clear it, make it defensible, and the two-day road
becomes two one-day roads.

> *"I can give you forty gold and a writ that says you're mine. I can't give you anyone to
> come with you. If that's not enough, say so now and I'll think better of you than if you
> say it out there."*
> — **Elara Dawnstrider**

## Terms

- **40 gp** on return, and the Preservers' writ — which is worth more than the coin at any
  crusader checkpoint between here and Kenabres.
- Anything found in the house is theirs to keep.
- **Elara** wants a written report. She is serious about this and will read it.

## Completion

The house is held, and word is sent back to Drezen. **Rothin Vald** at the Fane learns of it
by the next runner and starts using it within the week.
