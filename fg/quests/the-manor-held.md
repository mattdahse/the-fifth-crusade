**Six rooms and a roof, halfway along a road that had nothing on it.** This is the job Elara
actually paid for, and it is the smallest of the four awards because it is the one she asked for
out loud.

## Terms

- **40 gp** on return, and the Preservers' writ - worth more than the coin at any crusader
  checkpoint between here and Kenabres.
- **Elara wants a written report.** She is serious about this and will read it.

## Completion

Nothing hostile left in the manor, the breaches blocked or watchable, and **word sent back to
Drezen**. Rothin Vald at the Fane learns of it by the next runner and starts using it within the
week, which is the whole commission.

**The sealed room counts.** A party that clears the manor and leaves a laying queen behind a pile
of rubble has not made the place safe to sleep in, and Elara's couriers are the ones who will find
that out. Do not award this one until the north-east corner is dealt with.
