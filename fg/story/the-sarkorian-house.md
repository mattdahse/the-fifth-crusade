# The Sarkorian House

<!-- id: sarkorian_house -->
<!-- order: 1 -->

*The first commission. A 1st-level adventure for four, running one session.*

## What Elara Knows

The house is empty. She is wrong.

The company that sheltered there in Lamashan left it empty, and said so, and that was six
weeks ago. What has moved in since is a hand of the Ivory Labyrinth — two squatters and the
acolyte who keeps them — put there deliberately to count crusader traffic on the western road.

## What Is Actually Happening

The Labyrinth worked out something the crusade has not: that everything moving west from
Drezen now moves along one road, and that one house sits halfway along it. They are not
raiding. They are **counting**. The ledger in the house is three months of tallies, and the
last four entries are patrols.

That is the thread. This is a first job that looks like clearing a ruin and is actually the
party stumbling into the reason the road is about to become dangerous.

## Running It

**The approach.** The house is visible from a quarter mile in open country. There is no
sneaking up on it in daylight, and the squatters keep a watch that is honest in the morning
and not at all honest after noon.

**The fight.** [encounter: sarkorian_house] — 670 XP, a hard encounter at 1st level, and it
should feel like one. Fought indoors under a roof: no long bowshots, and the acolyte opens
with *burning hands* the moment two characters are adjacent. The rubble spill on the north
side is the tactical answer — difficult terrain, but it flanks the room and the squatters
will not think to guard it.

**The break.** The squatters are mercenaries. When the acolyte goes down, they run or they
surrender, and a party that takes a prisoner gets the ledger explained to them. A party that
kills everyone has to work it out on their own, which is slower and less interesting.

**The reward.** [parcel: sarkorian_house]

## What It Changes

This is the point of the whole campaign, so make it visible.

Hold the house and the road to the Sarian Fane becomes two one-day marches. Say so out loud
at the table. Then, the next time the party travels west, run it as two days instead of
three and let them notice.

**Rothin Vald** starts using the waystation within the week. **Chyrrik**, alone at the Fane
and carrying every message that crosses the Marchlands, gets a place to sleep at the halfway
point — which means the next time she comes through, she is not exhausted, and the party
meets a woman who is glad to see them.

## Hooks Out

- **The ledger** goes to **Mira Thistledance**, who pays for it, and who now has a reason to
  keep the party on retainer.
- **The tokens** — three hooked iron discs. Somebody in Drezen is handing these out.
- **Whoever the fourth hand in the ledger belongs to** never came back to the house. They are
  still out there, still counting.
