# Ability: Smite Good (fiendish)

<!-- id: ability_smite_good -->
<!-- order: 90 -->

Carried by any creature with the **fiendish** template. Linked from the NPCs that have it so the
rule is one click away at the table instead of a search through a book.

**Once per day, as a swift action**, the creature chooses one target it can see. If that target is
**good**, the creature adds its Charisma bonus to attack rolls and a bonus to damage equal to its
total Hit Dice against that target. Against a target that is not good, the smite is wasted.

The effect lasts until the target is dead or the creature rests.

## At the table

Two things make this easy to forget and worth not forgetting.

It is a **swift action**, so a drone smites and bites in the same round — there is no turn where
it visibly winds up. And it only works on a **good** target, which at this table means it lands on
a paladin or a cleric of Iomedae and does nothing at all to a neutral scout. That asymmetry is the
interesting part: the nest reliably picks the most righteous thing in the room and bites that.

For a 2 HD drone with Charisma 11 it is **+0 to hit and +2 damage**. Small. But four drones each
choosing the party's paladin adds up to eight extra damage on a character who has perhaps eleven
hit points, and that is the difference between a hard fight and a dead cleric.
