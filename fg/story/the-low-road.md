The rooms are in the book, C1 to C15, each with its own page, DCs and way through without a fight.
This record is how the whole thing moves.

## The shape of it

Three ways west from the knot, and the party chooses among them rather than being walked:

- **Through the gate** (C9): the Horn, then the Keep, the Keeper and the strongroom, then the cavern.
- **Through the midden** (C8): the Bishop, fed or fought, then the crawl, which comes up in the
  cavern's south end and never meets the gate at all.
- **Up and out through the old ways** (C6): the Tanner's ladder to Cinder Row, for a party that wants
  a roof, a barred door and eight hours. See the clock.

## The clock

**Morning of the 27th of Neth.** The cemetery at the foot of the cliff has mourners in it all day, so
nothing uses the ladder until dark. That is the only reason the Keeper is still here.

| When | What the Low Road does |
|---|---|
| The trapdoor opens | Tobin and Hanno at the stone door; Wendel, Lutz and Chin Whiskers at the knot; the Horn at the gate; the Keeper at his desk, writing the party down. |
| The knot falls or is passed | Somebody rings the bell. Each leader believes the other rang it. Neither comes. |
| The gate is passed | The Keeper, if he still has a door, casts *obscuring mist* and goes for the strongroom. |
| Dusk (about eight hours in) | The Keeper goes west with the strongbox and the ledger, alone, across the cavern and down the ladder. The porters go home. The Horn stays at the gate until somebody tells him the clerk has gone. |
| Nightfall | The ladder is hauled up from below by whoever is waiting at the foot, and the back door is shut from the other side. |

**A party that rests eight hours** in the Tanner's wakes at dusk, and meets the Keeper on the ledge
with the strongbox in both arms, or in the middle of the cavern with a darkmantle on his head.

## If they get away

- **The Keeper, west.** He reaches the manor two days on with the strongbox and the ledger, and Ysolde
  Karn has him in the cellar within the week. Put the ledger in the manor's store, and the sewn purse,
  opened, on the kitchen table.
- **The Horn, west**, sent or let go. He arrives at the manor looking for the men who sent the note
  and finds Ysolde's three. Add him to the Labyrinth post, and let him be the one who asks the party
  what happened to the others.
- **The relief detail, west.** Two days on the road with half their rations and no map, if Tobin lost
  his. Most of them will be at the manor when the party gets there, and they will remember.

## What the party's work changes

Say these out loud when they are true:

- **The back door is shut.** The ladder cut or carried off, and the people the Labyrinth walked in
  can no longer walk out.
- **The price of grain comes down.** The stores go back to Lupenor's Market and the board by the water
  pump is chalked lower within the week.
- **The South Bank has been searched**, for the first time, underneath: three trapdoors under three
  empty houses are on Mira's map.
- **Fourteen people** walked into Drezen under new names, and **two of them are in white tabards**.
  The register goes to Mira. It is the start of something, and not this chapter's.
- **The Diggers are laid to rest**, if the bones went over the ledge.
- **Somebody has to feed the Bishop now.**

## Scaling

**For three players,** run one initiate in the lodging, two rats, two centipedes and one darkmantle,
and pay the new objectives at 200 each. **For five,** add a third initiate at the knot, a fourth rat
and a third darkmantle. The Horn, the Keeper and the Bishop do not change: they are people, not
numbers.
