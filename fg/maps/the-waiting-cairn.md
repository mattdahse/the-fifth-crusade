**The last marker before the valley.** Open Marchlands at dusk: a low grassy rise with the cairn
on its crown, the broken square of an old sheepfold just downhill, and a dry stony wash along the
south edge. The party arrives from the **east** (the right edge), walking the markers out of
Drezen.

**The plate** is a 1536 × 1024 generation, trimmed 12 px top and bottom and reflect-padded 32 px
each side to **1600 × 1000**: **32 × 20 squares at 50 px, 160 × 100 ft**.

**What the art shows, in squares** (pixel ranges in brackets):

- **The cairn** (x 480–590, y 140–245): about two squares across and six feet high. Climbing it
  is a move action and Climb DC 10, and standing on it gives high ground. **It is not drawn as
  an occluder**, deliberately: Garth starts the fight standing on top of it, and a closed wall
  round him would blind him and hide him from everyone. Rule its cover at the table instead: a
  Medium creature behind it from you has total cover, a creature on top of it has none.
- **The sheepfold** (x 650–960, y 285–555): a six-square broken square of knee-high dry-stone
  walls. **Not occluders.** They give partial cover (+2 AC) to anyone crouched behind them, and
  stepping over one costs an extra 5 feet of movement. The south-east corner has collapsed into
  rubble, which is difficult terrain.
- **The dry wash** (the bottom quarter, roughly y 760 down): loose gravel and boulders,
  difficult terrain.
- **Thorn scrub**, three clumps (west, north-east, south-east): difficult terrain, and they hide a
  crouching wolf from anyone more than 30 feet away.

No occluders: nothing on this map stands taller than a man except the cairn, and that is
ruled at the table (above).
