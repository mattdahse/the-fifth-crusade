# C4. The Grain Store

<!-- chapter: The South Bank Job -->
<!-- section: The Low Road -->
<!-- order: 4 -->

A dug storeroom heaped to the roof props with sacks of grain, each one stencilled with the mark of
**Lupenor's Market**. There were sixty sacks. There are forty, and a good many of those are split.

> The heap smells of wheat and of something living in it. Grain has spilled across the floor in a
> fan from the split sacks at the back, and the fan is patterned with small, very busy footprints.
> Scratched into the wall above the heap, where the Labyrinth has not bothered to look, is a column
> of little marks in groups of five, and beside each group a tiny picture: a loaf, a turnip, a fish.

**Three dire rats** live in the sacks and fight for them, not for the room.

- **Without a fight:** smoke, a torch at the doorway, or a split sack dragged out into the tunnel.
  They follow the grain. Anything that makes the heap less comfortable than the dark sends them off
  into the old ways.
- **The bite carries filth fever**, DC 11. Note who is bitten.

**This was the slaves' larder.** The marks on the wall are theirs: seventy years of what they stole
from the demons' stores, one scratch a meal. **It is the other ledger in the Low Road, and the only
honest one.**

**The grain is the job nobody asked for.** It was walked out of Lupenor's warehouses along the
citadel wall two months running, and it is why the price on the board by the water pump in the
market was rubbed out and written in again three times this month. A hundredweight a sack; the
porters next door (C5) will carry it back for the same wage.

**Where it goes:** south to the knot; a short passage east to the packing room.
