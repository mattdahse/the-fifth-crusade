# A1. The Yard

<!-- chapter: The Southshore Job -->
<!-- section: The Yard -->
<!-- order: 1 -->

Beaten dirt behind a boarded fence, with a wide double gate standing open on the street. Seven
heaps of sorted scrap stand taller than a man with gaps between them, so the yard is a **maze of
cover** rather than an open lot. A handcart, a chopping block and woodpile, a water trough.

**The chopping block does not sit flat**, and has not for years.

> The block is a section of trunk about knee high, split across the top and black with use. It
> rocks when you lean on it. Somebody has driven a wedge under one side and the wedge is newer
> than the block.

- **Perception DC 15** while actually searching the yard, or **DC 22** in passing, to notice the
  rock and the wedge.
- **Strength DC 10** to lift the block clear; it is a hundredweight of wet oak.
- Underneath, a hole lined with a barrel end and a tarred canvas bundle in it.
- One question to **Sera** finds it for free (**Diplomacy DC 15**, or nothing at all once the
  children are safe). She has watched her father move it twice.

**The house has two ways in.** The **street door** opens off Cinder Row into the shop, at the
front, in full view of the road. The **yard door** is in the west wall of the living room, and
that is the one a party in the yard will use - it puts them in the middle of the house with the
children's room on their right and Hesk between.

**The gate** stands open and its hinges are dry — **Perception DC 5** to hear it move, which
means opening it quietly is **Disable Device DC 12** with oil or a rag, and a free alarm without.

**Two dogs** are loose in it after dark. They are the whole difficulty of the approach, and the one
problem here with a kind solution: they can be fed, calmed, avoided, or put down non-lethally.
**Killing them noisily is the loudest thing the party can do**, and it brings the house out.

- **Handle Animal DC 15**, or **wild empathy**, to quiet a dog that has not been struck.
- **DC 10 instead** if the party thought to bring meat, and this is worth rewarding generously.
- **Stealth vs. Perception +8** to cross the yard unnoticed — the heaps break line of sight, so
  give a **+2 circumstance bonus** to anyone moving heap to heap rather than straight across.
