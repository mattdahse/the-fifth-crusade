# C12. The Dressing Room

<!-- chapter: The South Bank Job -->
<!-- section: The Low Road -->
<!-- order: 12 -->

Off the lodging, a small dug room with two plank bunks, a basin, a writing table and a row of pegs
hung with city clothes. This is where people who come up the ladder are given an apron or a coat, a
trade and a name, before they are walked up through the scrapyard into Drezen. It is why, at the
yard on Cinder Row, sometimes more come out than went in.

> Clothes hang from the pegs as if people had just taken them off: a laundress's apron, a cooper's
> leather, a clerk's black coat, and two white tabards with a red sword on the breast. On the table
> there is a narrow book, a pot of ink and a stack of passes, and on the last peg a long blue scarf
> with small white stars along the edge.

**The register** is the second most important document in the Low Road. Fourteen arrivals since
Rova, each with a date, a name, a trade and a remark in the Keeper's hand. Most of the remarks are
unkind and some are funny. **The eighth and the eleventh read only *For the Preservers*.** Two
people the Labyrinth walked into Drezen are wearing white tabards in Iomedae's Preservers now.
*That is a thread for Mira, Elara and Cornelia, later. Let the party find it and carry it out.*

**The passes** are for the citadel gate, correct in every particular except the signature.

**Kora's scarf.** The long blue scarf with white stars along one edge, the third star picked half
loose, belongs to one of Kora Sjon's regulars: a widow who comes to the fortune-telling shop every
Starday for the cards, always asks for the seat by the window, and worries that star with her thumb
while the cards are turned. The register has her: *A widow, for the west end; comes and goes by the
ladder.* The window in Kora's shop looks out over the Marchlands and the western road. **What the
widow was watching from it is for later.** Kora knows the scarf at once.

**Where it goes:** south to the lodging. Nowhere else.
