<!-- chapter: The Sarkorian Manor -->

The map taken out of the South Bank tunnel ends on a Sarkorian ruin a day along the western road.
**Elara Dawnstrider** wants that ruin held: the road from Drezen to the Sarian Fane is two days on
foot with nothing on it, and a waystation halfway turns it into two one-day roads.

She believes it is empty. It is not. It is the Ivory Labyrinth's counting-post, and it stopped
counting three weeks ago. Its adept, **Ysolde Karn**, holds the whole house with the last two of
nineteen men, wears a crown with antennae on it, and believes she commands the fiendish hive under
the floor. She does not. A quasit called **Nib** does, invisibly, from her shoulder, and the two of
them are feeding the men to the brood and digging a tunnel toward Drezen.
