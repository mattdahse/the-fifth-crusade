# E5. The Table Room

<!-- chapter: The Sarkorian Manor -->
<!-- section: The Manor -->
<!-- order: 5 -->

A rough table and stools, and the **trapdoor** in the floor. Corwin's, and that is not an accident -
the way down to the hive is in the room he sleeps beside.

He does not stand in its doorway himself. The whole point of a longspear is that somebody else is
in it, so he stands back in the hall between his two doorways where he reaches either one.

- **The trapdoor is not locked and not hidden.** It is iron-banded oak with a ring pull:
  **hardness 5, 15 hp, Break DC 18** if somebody bars it from the far side.
- **Perception DC 15** to notice that the dust around the ring has been disturbed recently and
  often, which says the cellar is in use rather than forgotten.
