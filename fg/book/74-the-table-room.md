# E5. The Table Room

<!-- chapter: The Sarkorian Manor -->
<!-- section: The Manor -->
<!-- order: 5 -->
**Ysolde's room.** A rough table and stools, her bedroll on the floor beside the **trapdoor**, and
the copper crown's empty peg on the wall when she is wearing it, which is always.

**The table is the tunnel.** Chalked edge to edge in a careful hand: a long line running east from
a circle marked *the Mother*, with a tick and a figure for every week, and at the far edge of the
table a little drawing of a city wall with a gate in it. The line has nearly reached the edge of
the table. It is a quarter of the way across.

- **The trapdoor is not locked and not hidden.** It is iron-banded oak with a ring pull:
  **hardness 5, 15 hp, Break DC 18** if somebody bars it from the far side.
- **Perception DC 15** to notice the dust around the ring has been disturbed recently and often,
  and the drag marks from the hall.
