# E4. The Pallet Room

<!-- chapter: The Sarkorian Manor -->
<!-- section: The Manor -->
<!-- order: 4 -->
The old dormitory: straw pallets for nineteen, in rows, and nobody in any of them. Most still have
somebody's things at the head: a spare shirt, a carved spoon, a letter never sent. **The north
breach opens straight into it**, which makes it the easiest room in the building to walk into
uninvited. Garth and Tam went out this way.

**This is where Nib whispered.** On nights she was bored she went among the sleeping men and told
them things about the hatch. A character who sleeps here, or spends any time alone in this room
in the dark, may hear a very small voice say their name. **Nobody is there.** It is a good moment
to give somebody who has been dismissing Garth.
