# E3. The Store

<!-- chapter: The Sarkorian Manor -->
<!-- section: The Manor -->
<!-- order: 3 -->
Barrels and sacks, mostly emptied, and the gear of a digging crew: spoil baskets, a stack of
shoring timber, two shovels worn bright. The men haul what the ants dig up out of the cellar and
tip it into the ravine at night.

**The ledger is here**, on a shelf by the door. The front is more than a year of tallies of
everything that went west, stopping mid-page three weeks ago. **The back pages run the other way**,
under the heading *The Road*: feet dug, one figure a day, and at the end *Drezen, 22 miles less
1,240 ft.* and *four years*, underlined twice.

That ledger is the single most important object in the manor and it is easy to walk past.
