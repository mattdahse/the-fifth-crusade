# What the Work Changes

<!-- chapter: The Sarkorian Manor -->
<!-- section: Afterwards -->
<!-- order: 1 -->

Elara asked for a waystation on a two-day road. What is on offer is bigger, and the measure of the
session is which of these the party can say afterwards:

**The counting stops.** Ysolde's ledger goes back to Drezen and Mira Thistledance learns the
Labyrinth has been mapping crusader traffic for three months. That is worth more than the manor.

**The hive loses a generation.** Burning the clutch is the thing that is permanently different. A
hive that loses a season sends nothing up the tunnel next year.

**The manor is held.** Six rooms and a roof halfway along a road that had nothing on it. Rothin
Vald's runners start using it within the week, and that is the whole commission.

A party that gets one of the three has done a job. A party that gets all three has changed the
road. **Each is its own quest record and carries its own XP** - drag them onto the party sheet as
they land, rather than totalling anything at the end.
