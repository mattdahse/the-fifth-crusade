A natural sink in the rock that the slaves broke into seventy years ago and filled with the earth
they could not hide. The Labyrinth fills it with everything else. A ring of refuse round a black
pit, and the smell arrives a long way ahead of the room.

> The midden link opens onto a ledge of trodden muck round a pit. The refuse is heaped in a ring,
> broken crockery and rags and bones and rotten timber and something that might have been a
> cart-wheel, and in the middle, down in the dark, the heap moves. An eye comes up on a stalk and
> finds you. "Is it Sunday," the Bishop says.

**The Bishop** is an otyugh, CR 4, and not a fight unless somebody makes it one. **Any food at all**
makes it friendly; **Diplomacy DC 13** does the same. A sack of the stolen grain is a very good gift.
It asks after people forty years dead, and it wants to know whether the horned ones are gone. Tell
it they are. Then it asks who will feed it now, and that is a real question.

**It is also the third way west.** On the far side of the pit, the slaves' **crawl** runs north under
the rock to the cavern's south end: one square wide, a low roof (**difficult terrain**, and no Large
creature fits it, which is why the Bishop is still here). A party the Bishop likes goes through its
midden to the crawl and never meets the gate at all.

**The pit** is difficult terrain, and the Bishop's home; a character who goes into it is in reach of
everything the Bishop has. **The midden finds** take an hour's digging with its leave.

**The Labyrinth feeds it bodies.** That is one of the reasons people go into the scrapyard and do not
always come out. The Preserver's buckle in the finds is from a man who came down here asking
questions in Lamashan. Mira will want it. So will Elara Dawnstrider, who has not been told one of
her recruits is missing, because nobody noticed.

**Where it goes:** east along the midden link to the old ways; the crawl north to the cavern (C14).
