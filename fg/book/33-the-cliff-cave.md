The passage narrows and comes out in a cave in the cliff face, open to the air. A **coil of rope**
lies beside an iron spike driven into the rock, worn shiny in bands an arm's length apart.

> The passage ends in daylight. The cave mouth opens on nothing at all — sixty feet of air and
> then broken ground at the foot of Drezen's western wall. A coil of good hemp rope lies against
> the rock beside an iron spike, and the rope is worn shiny in bands an arm's length apart.

Lowered, it puts a careful climber on the ground outside Drezen's wall: no gate, no toll, no watch
officer writing a name in a book. **This is what the cell was actually for.**

- **Climb DC 10** with the rope made fast, **DC 25** on the bare rock, and it is a **60 ft. drop** —
  falling is 6d6.
- **Perception DC 12** to notice the wear bands and understand this has been in use for years
  rather than left here.
- **Survival DC 15**, or the marked map, to work out where the foot of the cliff comes out.

**Finding and mapping the route is worth 400 XP**, and it is the piece of intel Mira did not know
she was buying.
