# C4. The Cliff Cave

The passage narrows and comes out in a cave in the cliff face, open to the air. A **rope ladder**
is spiked to the stone at the lip and heaped on the floor beside it, ready to go over.

> The passage ends in daylight. The cave mouth opens on nothing at all — sixty feet of air and
> then broken ground at the foot of Drezen's western wall. Four iron spikes are driven into the
> stone at the lip, and a rope ladder is made fast to them and heaped in a coil on the cave floor,
> rungs and all, so that it can go over the edge at a word. The rungs are worn pale in the middle
> and dark at the ends.

Lowered, it puts anyone at all on the ground outside Drezen's wall: no gate, no toll, no watch
officer writing a name in a book. **This is what the cell was actually for.**

**Heaped, it shows nothing from below.** That is the point of keeping it coiled rather than
hanging, and it is why the cliff face has never given the route away — which is also why Mira's
watcher could see a yard and no more.

- **Kicking it over the lip is a move action** and it falls its full length in the same round.
  Hauling it back up is a full round, and can be done from the top with the ladder occupied.
- **No Climb check to go down it** at half speed, unencumbered, with nobody shooting. **Climb DC 5**
  in a hurry, in armour, or with a hand busy. It is a **60 ft. drop** if a check ever does fail,
  which is 6d6.
- **Climb DC 25** on the bare rock, which is the number that matters once the ladder is up or cut.
- **Perception DC 12** to read the wear on the rungs and understand this has been in regular use
  for years rather than left here.
- **Survival DC 15**, or the marked map, to work out where the foot of the cliff comes out.

**Cutting it down is a permanent change to the city.** Four spikes, a knife and about a minute, or
the whole thing carried off at 40 lb. Either way the Ivory Labyrinth's back door into Drezen stops
working, and that is worth saying out loud at the table when it happens — it is a bigger result
than the six men in the tunnel.

**Finding and mapping the route is worth 400 XP**, and it is the piece of intel Mira did not know
she was buying.
