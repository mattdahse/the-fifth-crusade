# G4. The Road East

<!-- chapter: The Sarkorian Manor -->
<!-- section: The Warren -->
<!-- order: 4 -->

The one straight passage in the warren. It leaves east, wide enough for a soldier, and every few
yards its walls are shored with planks and posts carried down from the house: the Labyrinth's men
did that, on Ysolde's orders, because the ground east of the manor is loose.

**This is the road.** It runs a quarter of a mile east under the Marchlands toward Drezen, and at
its head on any given night there are workers digging and a soldier standing over them. Walk it to
the end and the company can tell Elara exactly what it is.
