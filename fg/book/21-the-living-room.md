# B2. The Living Room

<!-- chapter: The Southshore Job -->
<!-- section: The House -->
<!-- order: 2 -->

Table, benches, a cold hearth, and the **trapdoor** in the floor, iron-banded with a ring pull.

**Hesk holds the doorway at the north end of this room** and does not leave it, because everything
he cares about is behind him. He opens with *cause fear* on whoever looks most dangerous, then
*bless* if his people are still standing, and he does not come forward to do either.

He is carrying the key to the strongbox on a thong round his neck rather than with the house keys,
which is the tell that the box under the counter is worth opening.
