# G5. The Queen's Chamber

<!-- chapter: The Sarkorian Manor -->
<!-- section: The Warren -->
<!-- order: 5 -->

The largest space under the house: a broad oval cavern the ants found and widened, its floor heaped
around a raised mound with eggs past counting. The queen lies on the mound. Workers tend the eggs
around her.

> The gallery opens and the warmth comes out to meet you, sour and sweet. The chamber beyond is
> bigger than the great hall above it. In the middle, on a mound of packed earth drifted deep with
> pale eggs, something the size of a cart horse lifts a head as broad as a shield. Her shell is
> bone-white going amber at the ridges, her abdomen swollen and pale and still growing. Around her
> the workers go on working.
>
> Two squared timber props stand in the chamber, one high on the far side and one low, thick posts
> with a crossbeam wedged hard against the roof. Somebody carried those down here. In the
> upper-left corner, loose gravel has run down the wall from the roof in a long grey fan.

**The roof is the whole room.** The chamber lies under the dry stony watercourse on the approach
plate, and what is over it is a few yards of loose gravel and boulders. The **two timber props**,
set by the Labyrinth's men when the ants broke into the cavity, are what is holding it up. The
thick earth column near the west wall is the ants' own and carries almost nothing.

**The queen fights over the clutch and will not step off it.** Ten feet of reach, from the mound,
and the props are well outside it: a company working at the props is not in her reach unless it
comes to her. **She calls the brood the first time she sees intruders**, and the soldiers come in
through the chamber's mouth, which is the company's only way out.

**This is where the adventure ends**, if the company lets it: see **Bringing Down the Roof**.
