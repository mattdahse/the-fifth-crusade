North of the hall, through a plank door: a cramped office with a plank floor and walls lined in
salvaged masonry out of the scrapyard's sorted pile. A desk, a chair, a shelf of ledgers, a cot, a
helm-stand with no helm on it, and a door in the north wall to the strongroom.

> A narrow man in a chain shirt much too big for him is standing behind the desk with a ledger held
> to his chest and a glaive leaning on his shoulder. He has been waiting for you for about an hour and
> is visibly put out that you are late. "Four," he says. "I have you down as four. May I have the
> names? For the column."

**Anselm does not fight.** He casts *obscuring mist* and runs, or he makes an offer, usually both at
once. Touch him and he surrenders. He will sell the Horn, the stores, the route, the manor and the
register for a door out and his ledger, and he will offer to put it in writing.

**He will try to hire the party.** Wages out of the strongbox, which is not his, to deal with "the
man at the gate, who has taken something that was not addressed to him." Mira offered them glory
and a steady paycheck. This is the paycheck without the glory.

**What is in the room** (the parcel has the objects):

- **The ledger.** Five columns, *Received*, *Sent West*, *Spoiled*, *The Bishop* and *Shrinkage*,
  and every one adds up, because the rats' share is hidden in the first two. The *Shrinkage* column
  is the citadel gate's own form. Asked where he learned it, he says everybody knows it is the
  proper form, which is not true of anybody who has not seen the citadel's book. *A thread for
  later: do not pull it now.*
- **The Helmsmith entry.** *Potions, four, bought, two hundred gold.* Diddle knows those four were
  stolen from the back room (see the lodging, C2). So the Keeper stole the potions and also kept the
  money he says he paid for them.
- **The note**, *Send men*, and **three unsent letters** asking the order for a ruling. **The framed
  letter** is the only one the order ever sent: the hymns for a feast day.
- **The packed bag**, under the cot. **Perception DC 10** to notice a bag packed for travel in the
  room of a man who says he is not going anywhere. It is the proof that ends the quarrel.
- **A book** on the shelf among the ledgers: *On Mines and Countermines*, a Kenabres printing on
  siege-mining and shoring. **Dorogh** has more books than a fighter is supposed to own, and this is
  one a man who reads load-bearing walls would want.

**The strongroom door** in the north wall is locked: **Disable Device DC 20**, or the right key off his
ring of fourteen, which takes **1d4 rounds** to find.
