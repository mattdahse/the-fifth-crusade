# G1. The Galleries

<!-- chapter: The Sarkorian Manor -->
<!-- section: The Warren -->
<!-- order: 1 -->

A passage leaves the nest going **down**, at a slope a man can walk, and opens into the warren: a
web of bored galleries and small round chambers, every one exactly as wide as the things that dug
it, running west to the queen and east toward Drezen. A year's work, and it is still going on.

> The passage widens and the noise arrives: a dry, continuous rustling, like wind in a field of
> dead grass, coming from everywhere at once. Then the galleries are full of them. Pale ants the
> size of dogs, going about in both directions, carrying earth, carrying grubs, carrying stones.
> They part around you and close up again behind. None of them look at you.

**The workers are the point of this map.** There are dozens of them and they are not a fight.
They are worth no XP and they never attack unless attacked or cornered. What they do is fill the
galleries: **every square with workers in it is difficult terrain**, and pushing straight through
one is a **DC 10 Acrobatics or Strength check**. And they are **tidy**: anything foreign left
lying in the warren gets carried to the larder. Read their record before running anything down
here.

**Soldiers come up out of the galleries**, not down the tunnel, when the queen calls. A party in
the queen's chamber has its only way out through the place her soldiers arrive from.

- **Perception DC 10** to hear the rustling from the nest, before anybody goes down.
- **Survival DC 15**, or simply following the workers carrying earth, finds the road east.
  Following the ones carrying eggs finds the queen.
