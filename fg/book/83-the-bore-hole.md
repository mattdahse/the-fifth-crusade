# F4. The Bore-Hole

<!-- chapter: The Sarkorian Manor -->
<!-- section: Below -->
<!-- order: 4 -->
At the north end of the nest chamber a shaft goes **up**: not bored like the rest of the tunnel,
but climbed, and by something much bigger than a soldier. It comes out in the sealed room in the
manor's north-east corner, where the queen laid her first clutch before it outgrew the room and
she came down it.

**This is also where Gell died.** His notebook says Nib offered to show him the best place to
stand. It was the foot of this shaft.

**It is a second way in and out** for a company that would rather not go through Ysolde's trapdoor:
a climb one creature wide, so anybody using it arrives alone and one at a time.

**The way on is down.** From the south side of the nest a passage leaves at a walkable slope, and
that is the way to the warren and the queen.
