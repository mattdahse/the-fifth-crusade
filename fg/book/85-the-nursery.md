# G2. The Nursery

<!-- chapter: The Sarkorian Manor -->
<!-- section: The Warren -->
<!-- order: 2 -->

A round chamber packed with grubs, laid out in rows as neat as loaves in a bakery, each one pale and
fat and as long as a man's arm, and shoulder to shoulder with them the eggs that will be the next
rows. Workers move up and down the rows feeding them.

**This is the next generation, and it is defenceless.** A torch or a flask of oil ends it in about a
minute, and the workers here will bite anybody who tries. That counts toward **The Clutch Burned**;
so does the queen's chamber coming down, which takes the nursery's supply with it.
