# E1. The Great Hall

<!-- chapter: The Sarkorian Manor -->
<!-- section: The Manor -->
<!-- order: 1 -->
The whole south half of the building, with the hearth in the west wall and a long broken table
down the middle - the only cover in the room.

**Ysolde holds court here**, from the hearth, with the two men standing where she can see them.
Every one of the five north rooms opens into the hall, so anybody crossing the house crosses it.

**Two breaches, and they are why this is not a fortress.** The north wall is open into the pallet
room; the east wall is open into the hall. Three people cannot watch both, and Ysolde does not try.

**The hearth's flue is cold**, and it has not been lit in a year. **Perception DC 15** at the
hearth notices small clawed tracks printed in the soot, going in and out of the chimney, like a
large bird's. Reach up the flue and there is a waxed leather satchel on a ledge of brick: **Gell's
satchel**. It is also the way Nib leaves when she has to.
