# E1. The Great Hall

<!-- chapter: The Sarkorian Manor -->
<!-- section: The Manor -->
<!-- order: 1 -->

The whole south half of the building, with the hearth in the west wall and a long broken table
down the middle - the only cover in the room.

**This is shared ground.** Every one of the five north rooms opens into it, both factions have to
cross it, and neither holds it. A parley happens here. So does a fight between the factions.

**Two breaches, and they are why this is not a siege.** The north wall is open into the pallet
room; the east wall is open into the hall. There is no front door to knock on and no wall to hold,
and both factions know it - which is exactly why neither has won.
