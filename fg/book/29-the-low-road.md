# The Low Road

Under the Dolvans' cellar is not a cellar. It is **the Low Road**: seventy years of tunnels dug in
secret, with spoons, hoe blades and one stolen pick, by the slaves the demons kept on the South
Bank. They used it to steal food, to hide children, to pray to anybody who might be listening, to
store weapons for a rising that never came, and, when a person could not bear another day of it,
to reach a cave in the cliff and go down into the Marchlands on a rope, preferring what was out
there to what was in here.

**The slaves dug a way out. The Labyrinth found a maze.** A small, badly connected cell of the
Templars of the Ivory Labyrinth now uses it the other way round: people in, and the city's grain
and silver out, west along the road to their counting-post at the manor. They believe the tunnels
are a sign from the Lord of Labyrinths. The slaves' marks at every junction, a small swallow
scratched low on the wall and pointing toward the cliff, they have decided are holy.

## Who is in charge

Nobody, which is the adventure. Three weeks ago a dying man came up the ladder from the manor with
a dead sergeant's horned helm and a note: *For the Master of the Drezen Way. Send men.* Nobody had
ever held that title, and now two men do.

- **Anselm Vauk, the Keeper**, claims it because he has the ledger, the keys and the strongroom.
- **Gerrit Bask, the Horn**, claims it because he put the helm on.

Nobody has been sent. They have not spoken since. The relief detail has been ordered west four
times, by two men, and countermanded by each. Everyone below the two of them is waiting to be told
which to obey, and both of them will try to hire the party against the other.

## Where everyone is when the party comes down

The fight upstairs was not quiet. By the time the trapdoor opens:

- **Tobin and Hanno** hold the stone door in the lodging (C2), on the Keeper's order.
- **Wendel and Lutz** are at the knot (C3), on the Horn's, with the bell-cord.
- **Chin Whiskers**, the scrapper who ran, went out of the house, down through the Tanner's
  trapdoor into the old ways, and told the Keep. He is sitting at the knot, bandaged.
- **The Horn** holds the gate (C9). **The Keeper** is in the counting room (C10), and has written
  the party into his ledger from Chin Whiskers' description: *Arrivals, unscheduled: four.*
- **The porters** are in the packing room (C5). **The Mole** is at the fall (C7). **The Bishop** is
  where it always is.

## The clock: dusk

It is the morning of the 27th of Neth. **Nobody uses the ladder by daylight**: the cemetery is at
the foot of the cliff, and it has mourners in it all day. So the Keeper cannot leave until dark,
and at dark he will: down the ladder with the strongbox and the ledger, leaving the Horn to hold
the gate. See the ledge (C15).

A party that goes back up to rest for eight hours wakes at dusk, and the Keeper is going over the
lip as they come down the stairs. A party that pushes on at 1st level catches him before he goes.
Both are good sessions. Say plainly, early, that the cell moves at night.

## The bell

A cord runs from the knot along the hall to a bell at the gate and on to the counting room. **One
pull means the Keeper wants you. Two means the Horn does. Nobody has agreed what three means.**
Whoever rings it, each of the two believes the other did, and neither comes: the Keeper does not go
toward trouble and the Horn does not leave the gate. It is the most elaborate way in the Low Road
of doing nothing, and a party that cuts it has done nothing either.

## In the dark

Three of the four can see without a light and every Labyrinth man carries one. **The party sees
them first**, at sixty feet, lit, and that is an advantage worth letting them feel. Kora needs a
light. The Keeper's answer is *obscuring mist*, which blinds darkvision as surely as eyes.

## The swallows

At every junction, a swallow the length of a thumb is scratched low on the wall, its head toward
the cliff. **Perception DC 12** to notice the first; after that the party sees them everywhere, and
cannot get lost. Emmet Rook knows what they are. The Labyrinth thinks they are maze-marks, and has
chalked a maze beside several of them, pointing the other way.
