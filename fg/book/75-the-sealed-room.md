# E6. The Sealed Room

North-east corner. The doorway is filled with fallen masonry and has been for years, and **the
queen did not come in through it.** She flew in through a hole in the roof, walled herself in, and
laid her first clutch here. When it outgrew the room she went down the bore-hole, and she has not
been back.

> The doorway is not a doorway any more. Dressed stone from the wall above has come down into it
> and settled, and the gaps between the blocks have silted up with seventy years of dust. Warm air
> comes through those gaps, and it smells sour and sweet at once.

**Behind the rubble** the room is packed earth on every surface, the floor deep in the split husks
of hatched eggs, and in the north-east corner the mouth of the **bore-hole**, going down. Nothing
lives here now. It is the first place the company can see how big this has become.

**Clearing the rubble takes about ten minutes of loud work** and everyone in the manor will know
it is happening.

- **Strength DC 15**, repeated, to shift the blocks; each success is roughly two minutes off.
- **Two characters working together** halve the time. Anyone with a crowbar or a pick gets **+2**.
- **The bore-hole** is a climb, one creature wide, down to the nest: a second way below that does
  not go through Ysolde's trapdoor.

**If the roof comes down** on the queen's chamber, this room's floor goes down the bore-hole with a
bang that shakes slates off the roof.
