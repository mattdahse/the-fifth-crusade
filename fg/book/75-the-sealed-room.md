# E6. The Sealed Room

<!-- chapter: The Sarkorian Manor -->
<!-- section: The Manor -->
<!-- order: 6 -->

North-east corner. The doorway is filled with fallen masonry and has been for years, and **the
queen did not come in through it.**

Clearing the rubble is perhaps ten minutes of loud work and everyone in the manor will know it is
happening. The other way in is up the bore-hole from the nest below, which is how she got here and
how her soldiers reach her - and it is a climb one creature wide, so anybody using it arrives
alone and one at a time.
