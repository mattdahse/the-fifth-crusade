# M2. The Back Room

<!-- chapter: Market Day -->
<!-- section: The Market -->
<!-- order: 2 -->

Within the hour **a porter** finds each of them separately. He gives no name, and recites what he
has been told to say: *"A friend would like to buy you a drink. The nightingale sings at the side
door."* He means the side door off the alley. He does not know what the nightingale is either.

Mira's office is at the back of the trading house, over the yard where the Outfitter's Counter is.
**Jory** is tied to a chair in the storeroom next to it. If the
party caught him, they carried him in; **if he escaped, Mira's people brought him in half an hour
ago**, and she tells it as though it were a daring capture.

> A narrow room with a good table in it and one wall covered in scraps of paper joined by lengths of
> red wool. The woman who springs up from behind the table is a half-elf with green eyes and brown
> hair threaded with fine silver chains, a lute on the chair beside her and a wax tablet in her
> hand. She has already poured four cups.
>
> *"You didn't know each other an hour ago. An* hour. *And nobody told you what to do. Nobody works
> for anybody. Do you know how rare that is?"*
>
> *"I need a team. Not guards, not the watch — people who belong to no one. An elite strike force."*
> A small, delighted breath. *"Spies. And saboteurs, when it comes to it. More wine?"*
> — **Mira Thistledance**

**She pitches the strike force, and then its name**: three candidates off her tablet, read aloud
with feeling, and she wants theirs. Write down whatever they suggest. The candidates, the codenames
and how the game runs are on her record.

**Jory tells it all**, in a flood: Kenabres, his dead master, the relief cart, the scrap yard on
**Cinder Row** where a big man with an axe laughed at him and sold him the crate for two coppers.
He knows nothing past the shop counter. He knows the yard is real, and that the man knew what he
meant.

**Then Jory, and Mira has an idea from a book:** send him back to Cinder Row as a *double agent*.
Let the party talk her out of it — he would be dead by morning — and she agrees at once. Then she
asks what they think should happen, and does it: the Preservers (he hangs), kept as "our man in the
candle trade", a cart south with a letter, or **Marit Olsk**, the Kenabres seamstress who knew
him, if she is waiting in the yard to ask for him. She will be sure it was her idea.

**If a centipede got away over the roofs**, a child was bitten in the next street. She is upset, and
then brightens: the child is fine, and it is exactly what her network is for.

Then she puts a scrap-yard address on the table, taps it, and announces — slightly too loudly, for a
secret — that their first mission is tonight.

**Say what changed, out loud:** who in the market is alive, who they owe, and that four strangers
are now a company.
