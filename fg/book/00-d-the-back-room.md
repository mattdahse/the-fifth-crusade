# M2. The Back Room

<!-- chapter: Market Day -->
<!-- section: The Market -->
<!-- order: 2 -->

Within the hour **a porter** finds each of them separately. He gives no name. A woman would like
to buy them a drink, and would rather they came in by the side door off the alley, not the front.

Mira's office is at the back of the trading house, over the yard where the Outfitter's Counter is.
**Jory** is tied to a chair in the storeroom next to it. If the
party caught him, they carried him in; **if he escaped, Mira's people brought him in half an hour
ago**, and she says so without making anything of it.

> A narrow room with a good table in it and nothing else worth stealing. The woman behind the table
> is a half-elf with green eyes and brown hair threaded with fine silver chains, and there is a
> lute on the chair beside her. She has already poured four cups.
>
> *"You didn't know each other an hour ago. I watched you work that out."*
>
> *"There were four people in that market who went toward it, and not one of them works for
> anybody. That is rarer in Drezen than you would think, and it is exactly what I need tonight."*
> — **Mira Thistledance**

**Jory tells it all**, in a flood: Kenabres, his dead master, the relief cart, the scrap yard on
**Cinder Row** where a big man with an axe laughed at him and sold him the crate for two coppers.
He knows nothing past the shop counter. He knows the yard is real, and that the man knew what he
meant.

**Mira decides Jory's fate in front of them, and asks them first.** Handed to the Preservers, he
hangs. Kept, he is Mira's, and she does not call that mercy. Put on a cart south with a letter, he
lives somewhere else. Let the party's answer move her.

**If a centipede got away over the roofs**, a child was bitten in the next street. She says so, as a fact,
and moves on.

Then she puts a scrap-yard address on the table between them.

**Say what changed, out loud:** who in the market is alive, who they owe, and that four strangers
are now a company.
