Written for **four characters at 1st level on the medium track.** With the Low Road under it this job
is **11,455 across the party**; with Market Day in front of it (1,285) they reach the manor at
**12,740, about 3,190 each**. They cross into 2nd level about halfway through the Low Road, usually
somewhere between the old ways and the gate, and the manor's own 1,750 each leaves them just short
of 3rd, which is where the manor was always meant to leave them.

**Encounters, 7,605.** The yard 800, the house 1,400; below, the lodging 400, the knot 400, the grain
store 405, the old ways 600, the Diggers 400, the midden 1,200, the gate 600, the Keeper 600 and the
cavern 800. **Every one pays however it is overcome**: talked past, fed, bargained with or bled.

**Objectives, 3,850**, each its own quest record so it goes onto the party sheet when it is earned.
They are 34% of the job, above the module's usual 27%, because the two upstairs awards were raised
when the old tunnel and cavern were cut and have stayed raised with the table already in the house.

actions, and a party never told those were worth anything learns the wrong lesson about what this
campaign values.
