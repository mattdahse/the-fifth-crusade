# C11. The Strongroom

<!-- chapter: The South Bank Job -->
<!-- section: The Low Road -->
<!-- order: 11 -->

A masonry closet three squares on a side behind a locked door, and in it one iron-bound chest. This
is what goes west: the city's silver, a cut of every theft, and the pay for the manor.

> The chest is iron-bound oak with a good lock, and it is heavy. Somebody has chalked a maze on the
> lid, and then, below it, in the same hand, a list: *Strongbox. Ledger. Boots.*

- **The door: Disable Device DC 20**, **hardness 5, 20 hp, Break DC 23**, a minute of noise, or the
  Keeper's key, in 1d4 rounds on a ring of fourteen.
- **The chest: Disable Device DC 20**, **hardness 5, 15 hp, Break DC 23.** It weighs sixty pounds
  full, which is why the Keeper has waited for dark rather than carried it himself down a ladder in
  daylight, and why a party that meets him on the ledge meets him with both hands full.

**What is in it** (the parcel): coin, four crusade-stamped silver bars with the stamps half filed off
(the same work as the six in Hesk's strongbox), a sewn purse for *Y. K., the halfway house*, and two
potions of cure light wounds.

**Diddle's potions again.** Both vials are tagged *H.E. 214* in the same small hand, with the same
flask. They are the other two from the night the shutter was forced.

**The purse for Ysolde Karn** says where the money was going and to whom. It is the second thing in
the Low Road, after the marked map, that points at the manor.
