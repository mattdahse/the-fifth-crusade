# C13. The Chapel

South of the hall, a dug room the slaves made into a chapel for anyone who might be listening. Two
plank benches, a block of stone for an altar, and every god in the world scratched into the walls.

> The walls are covered in marks, floor to roof, hundreds of them, scratched with nails and spoons
> and bits of bone: a sword, a sun, a butterfly, a bow, a spiral, a hammer, a key, a bird with its
> wings out, and many you do not know, some of them a single line, done by somebody who could not
> remember what their god looked like but wanted to be counted. Across all of it, cut deep and new,
> is a maze. On the altar, a goat's head inside a five-pointed star.

**Every god, just in case.** The slaves prayed here for seventy years to all of them at once, on the
reasoning that one of them might be listening. None of them came. The Labyrinth has carved Baphomet's
maze across the lot and set his goat-headed star on the altar, and it believes that settles the
question of who was listening.

**Baphomet.** The company learned his symbols in the citadel library: the goat's head in the
pentacle, the two backward curves, the labyrinth. **All three are here**, and **Knowledge (religion)
DC 10** names him. Nobody has yet said the word Baphomet out loud inside the Dolvans'
house. This is where it gets said.

**Kora.** Among the marks, low on the east wall, is one she will know: the thundercloud of **Hei
Feng**, scratched small and careful. Somebody from very far away prayed to her god in here.

**The cache under the altar.** **Perception DC 18** to see that the altar stone has been lifted and set
back, many times, a long time ago. **Strength DC 15** to lift it; two can help. Emmet Rook knows it is
there and will say so. Under it is what the slaves put by for a rising that never came: cold iron,
hammered out in secret, the one metal that bites a demon. The Labyrinth carved its maze on the stone
above it and never looked underneath.

**Where it goes:** north to the hall.
