# D1. The Approach

<!-- chapter: The Sarkorian Manor -->
<!-- section: The Approach -->
<!-- order: 1 -->

Four hundred yards of open country. **There is no road** - whatever was built here was erased
generations ago by demons, weather and armies going both ways, and "the road" is the name of a
direction rather than a thing anybody can stand on.

The ground is not uniform and which way they come should be a real choice: a **ravine** shuts off
the west entirely, a **copse** of leafless trees in the north-east is the only cover tall enough
to hide a standing man, a **dry stony watercourse** runs down from the east low enough to move
along unseen if they are willing to be slow, and a scatter of **village foundations** knee-high
across the open ground is the only other cover there is.

The manor sits in the middle of it under a slate roof with two holes in it. **Two chimneys at the
west end** mark the kitchen oven and the great hall's hearth.
