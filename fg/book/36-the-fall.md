The Bakehouse branch ends in a dug room whose far half is filled to the roof with a spill of rock
and splintered timber. Forty years ago it came down on six diggers. Emmet Rook was the seventh, and
got out.

> An old man is on his knees in the rubble with a pick across his thighs, working a stone loose the
> way you would unpick a knot. A lantern by his knee is turned so low it is barely lit. At the edge
> of the spill, where he has cleared it, something pale shows between two stones, and then another
> something, and you understand that he has not been clearing toward a house.

**Emmet Rook** is the living history of the Low Road and the easiest person in it to talk to. Ask his
name and he will tell you. See his record for what he knows and what turns him.

## The Diggers (haunt, CR 1)

**400 XP.** Chaotic neutral haunt, 20-foot radius, the rubble end of the room.
**Caster level** 1st. **Notice: Perception DC 12**, to hear the picks.
**hp** 2. **Weakness:** positive energy. **Trigger:** proximity, a living creature crossing the
middle of the room. **Reset:** 1 day.

> Behind the rubble, somebody is digging. You can hear the picks, six of them, not in time, and
> somebody laughing, and then the roof groans, and it is not the roof of this room, and it comes
> down.

**Effect.** Everyone in the area is buried, as far as they can tell: **Will DC 12** or be panicked
for 1d4 rounds, fleeing back up the branch, and fatigued afterwards. Nothing falls. Emmet Rook does
not move; he has heard it every day for a month.

**Destruction.** Carry the bones out of the rubble, through the Low Road to the ledge (C15), and let
them go over into the Marchlands, which is where the six were going. Emmet knows this without being
told, and will say their names as they go. Positive energy neutralises it for a day: Kora's channel,
or a cure spent on the place.

**Kora.** Her curse draws the unquiet. If she is in the room when the haunt triggers, one of the six
does not leave with the others: a young one, a digger who liked a joke, who follows her after that
and moves small things where she will find them. He goes quiet if the bones go over the ledge.
Whether he goes quiet for good is up to her.

**Riven's lantern.** Emmet's lantern was broken once and mended with a copper collar and three rivets
in a triangle, the heads peened flat, the copper worked smooth so it will not catch in a pack.
**Riven Maelus knows that mend.** It is the way his father did it, and the only person he has ever
seen do it that way. Ask Emmet and he says an arrival traded it to him for bread in Lamashan, a man
who came up the ladder from the west and went on into the city. He did not ask the man's name. *This
is a hook, not an answer: strike it, or keep it for later, as you like.*

**Where it goes:** back north-east to the split. The Bakehouse is somewhere beyond the rubble, and
it is not coming.
