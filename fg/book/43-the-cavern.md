# C14. The Cavern

Natural, irregular, far wider than any tunnel, with fallen rock and stalagmites. Nobody dug it and
nobody likes it. The hall comes in from the east; the crawl from the midden comes up at its south
end; a passage west leads to the ledge.

**Two darkmantles are on the ceiling** and have been the whole time. They do not appear in the
initiative order until they drop.

**Everybody down here already knows.** Three weeks ago the relief detail tried to leave, crossed with
torches, and the ceiling took Hanno's cousin. That is why the relief never went. Hanno will tell
anyone who asks; the Bishop knows the roof has mouths; Emmet Rook knows what the slaves did about it.

**What the slaves did: you send a sack across first.** A sack, a bundle, anything the size of a
person thrown or dragged into the middle of the cavern with a light on it: the darkmantles drop on it,
and are on the floor, clumsy (speed 20) and visible, and a party waiting at the edge gets them there.
It is the whole trick, and it is worth a great deal of XP not to have to learn it the other way.

**Knowledge (dungeoneering) DC 11** names them and their habits; Dorogh has it at +8. **Perception DC
20** to spot one before it drops, **+5 for anybody who says they are looking up**.

**They drop together**, as a party reaches the middle: one on whoever carries the light and one on
whoever is behind them. In this party that is Kora, who needs a light, and whoever walks behind her.
A grabbed character is being strangled on the initiative count: **CMD 13** to escape, **constrict
1d4+4** every round they do not. **Blindsight 90 ft.** means putting the lantern out helps them, not
the party. Say so the first time somebody reaches for it.

**At dusk** the Keeper crosses here with the strongbox and a lantern, alone or behind the Horn if the
Horn is still with him. The ceiling does not care who it drops on. A party chasing him to the ledge
may arrive to find him in the middle of the cavern with a darkmantle on his head, which is its own
kind of decision.
