# A2. The Kennel

<!-- chapter: The Southshore Job -->
<!-- section: The Yard -->
<!-- order: 2 -->

A low plank kennel at the south-west corner with a chain staked beside it. The chain is long enough
to reach the gate and it is not on either dog tonight.

Nothing is hidden here. It is worth describing because a party that sees a kennel and a slack chain
has been told there are dogs before it meets them, and that is a fair warning given for free.
