West of the knot the hall runs straight, three squares wide, to a barricade of crates stacked
across it with one square left open at the south end. Beyond it is the Keep: the Labyrinth's inner
rooms, and the Keeper.

> The crates are stacked to shoulder height, and in the gap at the end of them stands a very big man
> in scale mail with a great horned helm down over his head like a bucket. The horns clear the
> roof by a finger. He is holding a greataxe upright in front of him in both hands, and he has
> plainly been standing like that for some time. "Nobody," the helm says, a moment after he does,
> "is to pass without the Master's word." A pause. "I am the Master."

**The barricade:** crates, climbable (**Climb DC 10**, a move action, and whoever tops it is flat-footed
to the Horn until they are down). They block sight. The bell hangs on the gatepost; see the bell.

**The Horn holds the gap and nothing else.** He will not come out past the crates and he will not
pursue. He cannot lie. Ask him anything and he answers.

**Three ways past him without a fight:**

- **The bag.** Tell him the Keeper is packed to go down the ladder at dusk with the strongbox and
  leave him holding the gate, or better, show him. He goes to arrest the clerk himself, and the
  party can walk behind him.
- **Call him Master.** Mean it. He lets them through "to deal with the clerk," and follows.
- **Give him his fight.** One champion against him, to a yield, and he keeps the terms: at 8 hit
  points or fewer he kneels and takes the helm off. Dorogh is the obvious candidate and the Horn
  will say so, because it is the fair fight.

**In a fight** he is dangerous: a greataxe on a first-level party kills people. He goes for the
biggest one first. But the helm blinds him to either side, **+2 to anyone attacking from a side or
rear square**, and knocking it off (**disarm or dirty trick, CMD 16**) costs him his next turn picking
it up.

**The quarrel from this side.** He wants to be sent: to lead the relief west and bring the manor's
men home. A party that lets him go is sending a big honest man to Ysolde Karn, who will be very
glad of him.

**Where it goes:** east to the knot; west along the hall to the counting room (C10, north), the chapel
(C13, south), and the cavern.
