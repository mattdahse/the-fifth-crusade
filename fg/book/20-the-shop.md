# B1. The Shop

<!-- chapter: The Southshore Job -->
<!-- section: The House -->
<!-- order: 1 -->

The whole south end of the house, and the way in from the street. Counters, shelves of sorted
metal, a strongbox under the till.

This is where the party arrives and where the fight starts. Three scrappers use a room they know in
the dark, breaking line of sight behind the counters rather than standing and trading.

**The house is one encounter.** It is a small space and it is open from room to room: a shout in
the shop is heard in the children's room, and nobody in this building fights alone. Hesk falls
back to the doorway at the north end of the living room the moment the shop is entered.

**A hinged board under the counter** looks like part of the counter until somebody pulls on it.

> The counter is a slab of ship timber on trestles, and the underside of it has been boxed in
> with planking at some point by somebody competent. One board sits a half-inch proud of its
> neighbours. Behind it, bolted through the floor joists, is an iron box the size of a bread crock.

- **Perception DC 18** to find the board. **DC 12** if the party has already taken Hesk's neck key
  and is looking for what it opens.
- **The strongbox: hardness 10, 30 hp, Break DC 26.** It is bolted down, so it cannot be carried
  off — a party that wants it open, opens it here, loudly.
- **The lock is good work: Disable Device DC 30.** Hesk's key is the intended answer and it is
  worn on a thong round his neck rather than with the house keys, which is the tell.
- Smashing it takes about a minute of hammering and can be heard through the floor, which matters
  a great deal if the tunnel below has not been dealt with yet.

> The box holds a man's whole life in coin, six thumb-sized bars of silver with the crusade stamps
> filed half off, two healing potions wrapped in rag so they would not knock together, and a
> tenancy sealed twice. Underneath all of it, in a twist of oiled cloth, is a plain iron ring that
> is not a scrap dealer's ring and that he did not buy.
