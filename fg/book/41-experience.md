Written for **four characters at 1st level on the medium track** - 2,000 XP each, 8,000 across the
party, which is exactly what this adds up to if they finish it.

**Combat, 5,800.** The yard 800, the house 1,400, the tunnel 1,200, the cavern 2,400.

**Objectives, 2,200.** Each is its own quest record, carrying its own XP, so it can go onto the
party sheet the moment it is earned rather than being totalled up at the end.

@link quest: intel_to_mira | The intel delivered (800 XP)
@link quest: the_dolvans_alive | The Dolvans taken alive (800 XP)
@link quest: the_cliff_route | The cliff route (400 XP)
@link quest: the_yard_watched | The yard watched first (200 XP)

**Award the objectives even if the party does not ask.** Two of them reward decisions rather than
actions, and a party never told those were worth anything learns the wrong lesson about what this
campaign values.

For **three players**, drop the cavern to four darkmantles and the objectives to 1,400 - intel
600, Dolvans 500, route 200, scouting 100. For **five**, run all six darkmantles as one wave and
add a seventh initiate.
