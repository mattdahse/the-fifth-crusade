Written for **four characters at 1st level on the medium track.** On its own this job is **6,750
across the party**, about 1,690 each; with Market Day in front of it (1,285) they reach the manor
at **about 2,010 each, just over 2nd level**, which is what the manor is written for.

**Combat, 3,800.** The yard 800, the house 1,400, the tunnel 800, the cavern 800.

**Objectives, 2,950.** Each is its own quest record, carrying its own XP, so it can go onto the
party sheet the moment it is earned rather than being totalled up at the end. They are **44%** of
this job, well above the module's usual 27%: they were raised when the tunnel and the cavern were
cut, so that the party still reaches 2nd level, and they were raised on decisions, not kills.

**Award the objectives even if the party does not ask.** Two of them reward decisions rather than
actions, and a party never told those were worth anything learns the wrong lesson about what this
campaign values.

For **three players**, run three initiates and one darkmantle, and pay the objectives at 1,800 -
intel 600, Dolvans 700, route 300, scouting 200 - about 2,000 each with Market Day. For **five**,
add a fifth initiate and a third darkmantle.
