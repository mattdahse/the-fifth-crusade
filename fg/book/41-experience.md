# The Experience Budget

<!-- order: 2 -->

Written for **four characters at 1st level on the medium track** - 2,000 XP each, 8,000 across the
party, which is exactly what this adds up to if they finish it.

**Combat, 5,800.** The yard 800, the house 1,400, the tunnel 1,200, the cavern 2,400.

**Objectives, 2,200.** The intel delivered to Mira 800, Alia and the children taken alive 800, the
cliff route found and mapped 400, the yard scouted before the strike 200.

**Award the objectives even if the party does not ask.** Two of them reward decisions rather than
actions, and a party never told those were worth anything learns the wrong lesson about what this
campaign values.

For **three players**, drop the cavern to four darkmantles and the objectives to 1,400. For
**five**, run all six darkmantles as one wave and add a seventh initiate.
