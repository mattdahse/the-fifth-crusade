# The Dolvans

<!-- chapter: The Southshore Job -->
<!-- section: Introduction -->
<!-- order: 3 -->

**Hesk Dolvan** kept a scrap yard through the occupation, and that is the whole of him: a man who
took the Ivory Labyrinth's maze in Kenabres twenty years ago, came north, and was useful enough to
the wrong people that they let him go on trading while Drezen belonged to demons.

The crusade arrived two months ago. It has not yet reached Cinder Row.

**Alia** wears the maze because he does. **Sera** is twelve and knows what the cellar is. **Corin**
is nine and does not. There is a baby in a crib.
