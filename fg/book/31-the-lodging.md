The relief detail's quarters: a dug tunnel three squares wide, shored with timber, four bedrolls
along the walls, a crate for a table and a fire ring gone cold. Four packs stand made up by the
bedrolls, and have stood there for three weeks.

> Two young men in mail too big for them stand at the stone door with swords out and bucklers up,
> the way they were shown. A lantern hangs from a prop behind them. One of them is saying, not
> quietly, "He said *hold* it, he didn't say what for," and the other one says, "Which he?"

**Tobin and Hanno** hold the door because the Keeper told them to. Wendel and Lutz went to the knot
because the Horn told them to. Neither pair is sure the other got it wrong.

**Without a fight:** an order given in the right voice makes them stand aside and then argue about
whether it was the right voice. **Intimidate DC 11** from anybody who has just come down the stairs
from the fight upstairs makes one surrender outright; when one gives up, both do. The first real
wound ends it the same way.

**Tobin carries the marked map**, in his shirt, because he has the best boots. He cannot read it.

**Hanno knows what is on the cavern ceiling.** It took his cousin three weeks ago, the first time the
relief tried to leave, and it is the real reason nobody has gone west since. He will tell anyone who
asks where they are going.

**The props are two generations.** **Knowledge (engineering) DC 10**: the old ones, black and
seventy years dry, were cut from bedsteads and floorboards and fitted by people who knew what they
were doing. The new ones are sorted lumber out of the scrapyard's pile, set by people who did not.

**Diddle's potions.** The relief detail's two potions carry paper tags reading *H.E. 211* and *H.E.
212*, each with a tiny drawing of a flask. **Diddle Scribes knows the hand: it is his.** *H.E.* is
Helmsmith Elixirs, the numbers are his stock numbers, and the flask is the mark he puts on
everything he bottles. That run went missing from the back room one night in Lamashan when the
shutter was forced, and James Helmsmith did not report it, because nobody in Drezen comes out for
less than a body. The Keeper's ledger (C10) says they were *bought*.

**Where it goes:** the stone door east to the cellar; the tunnel west to the knot; a short passage
north to the dressing room (C12).
