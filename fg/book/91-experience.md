# The Experience Budget

Written for **four characters arriving at 2nd level on the medium track**, which is where the
Southshore job leaves them.

**Combat, 5,075.** The Labyrinth post 805, the Locust-Sworn 670, the ant nest 2,400, the young
queen 1,200.

**Objectives, 1,925.** The manor held 400, the ledger delivered 600, the clutch burned 500, the
truce turned 425.

**7,000 across the party, 1,750 each**, which takes them from 2,000 to 3,750. That is most of the
way to 3rd level and deliberately not all of it - **the manor does not level them.** Third level
is 5,000 on the medium track and the remaining 1,250 belongs to whatever Mira does with the ledger.

Note the ratio, because it is the design and not an accident. Objectives are **27% of the total**,
the same share they were in the Southshore. Three of these four reward a decision rather than a
kill, and only one of them is the job anybody was actually hired for.

**Award them even if the party does not ask**, and say what each was for. A party never told that
burning a clutch of eggs was worth more than the boss standing over it learns the wrong lesson
about what this campaign values.

For **three players**, drop the nest to three soldiers and the objectives to 1,400 - manor 300,
ledger 400, clutch 400, truce 300. For **five**, add a fourth squatter to the Labyrinth post and a
third Locust-Sworn.

**If both factions are fought at once** the party earns 1,475 in one fight and probably does not
survive it. That is not a scaling option, it is a consequence, and the truce award exists to make
the alternative visible.
