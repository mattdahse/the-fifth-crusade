Written for **four characters arriving at 2nd level on the medium track**, which is where the
South Bank job leaves them.

**Combat, 4,870.** The halfway house 670 (Ysolde and two men), Nib 600, the ant nest 2,400, the
young queen 1,200. The Waiting Cairn on the road is another 1,200 and is not counted here.

**Objectives, 2,000.** The manor held 400, the ledger delivered 600, the clutch burned 500, the
road under the road 500.

**6,870 across the party, about 1,720 each**, which takes them from 2,000 to roughly 3,700. That
is most of the way to 3rd level and deliberately not all of it - **the manor does not level them.**
Third level is 5,000 on the medium track, and the rest belongs to the road and to whatever Mira
does with the ledger.

**Award Nib's XP however she leaves.** Killed, captured, bargained with or chased off up the
chimney: the company beat her, and the award is for that. A Nib who escapes comes back later, and
is worth it again.

**Award the objectives even if the party does not ask**, and say what each was for. A party never
told that burning a clutch of eggs was worth more than the boss standing over it learns the wrong
lesson about what this campaign values.

For **three players**, drop the nest to three soldiers and the objectives to 1,500 - manor 300,
ledger 450, clutch 375, road 375. For **five**, add a third man to the halfway house and a fifth
soldier to the nest.
