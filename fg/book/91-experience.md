<!-- order: 2 -->Written for **four characters arriving at 2nd level on the medium track**, which is where the
South Bank job leaves them.

**Combat, 4,870.** The halfway house 670 (Ysolde and two men), Nib 600, the ant nest 2,400, the
young queen 1,200, **however she dies**, under the roof included. The workers are worth nothing.
The Waiting Cairn on the road is another 1,200 and is not counted here.

**Objectives, 2,500.** The manor held 400, the ledger delivered 600, the clutch burned 500, the
road under the road 500, the roof brought down 500.

**7,370 across the party, about 1,840 each**, which takes them from 2,000 to roughly 3,850. That
is most of the way to 3rd level and deliberately not all of it - **the manor does not level them.**
Third level is 5,000 on the medium track, and the cairn and whatever Mira does with the ledger
take them the rest of the way.

**Award Nib's XP however she leaves.** Killed, captured, bargained with or chased off: the company
beat her. A Nib who escapes comes back later, and is worth it again.

**Award the objectives even if the party does not ask**, and say what each was for. A party never
told that burning a clutch of eggs was worth more than the boss standing over it learns the wrong
lesson about what this campaign values.

For **three players**, drop the nest to three soldiers and the objectives to 1,875 - manor 300,
ledger 450, clutch 375, road 375, roof 375. For **five**, add a third man to the halfway house and
a fifth soldier to the nest.
