Neither faction can take the manor without losing enough people that what is under the floor
finishes the rest. So they have split it, and nobody crosses, and nobody has for three weeks.

**Corwin is waiting for something to happen to Ysolde.** That is his entire strategy, and it is
why he will not attack armed strangers who come in through a breach: he lets them in, tells them
where the Labyrinth sleeps, offers to stay out of it, and means every word - right up until the
Labyrinth is dead and the party is standing between him and the sealed room.

**Ysolde will not deal**, because she believes the hive is hers and Corwin is a thief. She is
wrong about the first part in a way that gets people killed.

**The shape to run for.** A party that walks in swinging fights six people at once and probably
loses. A party that talks fights three, then three, on ground of its own choosing. A party that is
clever gets the two factions to fight each other and walks into the sealed room while it happens.
