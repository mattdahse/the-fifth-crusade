# F1. The Cellar

<!-- chapter: The Sarkorian Manor -->
<!-- section: Below -->
<!-- order: 1 -->

Down the trapdoor: a dry stone room the house was built on. Shelving down long ago, barrels still
where they fell, a hearth-cold smell of dry stone and something sourly sweet under it.

**The west wall is not sound.** A spill of fallen masonry is heaped against it, and the wall behind
the heap is dressed and mortared and newer than the rest. The rubble hides a **door**, and the
rubble has been moved before and put back: there is a drag mark on the flagstones from the foot of
the stair to the heap, and it is not one drag mark - it is a year of them, laid over each other.
