# C1. The Cellar

<!-- order: 1 -->

Down the trapdoor: mortared stone, a flagstone floor, crates and barrels, and an empty shelf rack
against the west wall.

**The trapdoor is chained.** Hesk put the chain on three weeks ago, the night the Horn came up the
stairs in the helm looking for the privy and found the children's room instead.

> An iron chain runs through the ring pull and round a staple in the floor joist, and a padlock
> the size of a fist holds it. The links are bright. The staple has been driven in recently: there
> is still fresh splintered wood round it.

- **The key** is on Hesk Dolvan's ring of house keys, not on the thong round his neck, which is the
  strongbox. Alia and Sera both know which one it is.
- **The padlock: Disable Device DC 20.** **The chain: hardness 10, 5 hp, Break DC 26.** Breaking
  it is a minute of noise that the lodging below hears.
- **The staple** can be levered out of the joist with a crowbar or a scrap hook: Strength DC 15,
  two can help, and it takes a round.

**The secret door is behind the rack**, and it is not secret to the two men behind it.

> The shelf rack against the west wall is empty and has been for a while, and the dust on the
> flagstones in front of it is scuffed in an arc, the way a floor wears where something heavy
> swings open and shut.

- **Perception DC 20** to find the door, **DC 15** searching the rack itself. **Free** from Alia,
  Sera or Hesk.
- **The door is stone on a pivot and it is locked** from the lodging side with a bar. **Strength DC
  18** to force it (it grinds), **Disable Device DC 20** to lift the bar through the gap with a
  blade. **Knocking works.** Tobin and Hanno have been waiting an hour for somebody to tell them
  what is happening, and an answer in the right voice opens it from their side.
- **Nobody opens it quietly by force.** Tobin and Hanno are on the other side either way.
