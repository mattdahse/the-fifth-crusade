# C1. The Cellar

<!-- chapter: The Southshore Job -->

Down the trapdoor: mortared stone, a plank floor, crates and barrels, and a shelf rack against the
west wall.

**The secret door is behind the rack.** Finding it is a search of the shelving rather than of the
whole room, and Alia or Hesk will simply say where it is - she is the fastest route to everything
under this house and she will trade all of it for the children.
