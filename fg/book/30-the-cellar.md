Down the trapdoor: mortared stone, a plank floor, crates and barrels, and a shelf rack against the
west wall.

**The secret door is behind the rack.**

> The shelf rack against the west wall is empty and has been for a while, and the dust on the
> flagstones in front of it is scuffed in an arc, the way a floor wears where something heavy
> swings open and shut.

- **Perception DC 20** to find the door, or **DC 15** if the party searches the rack specifically
  rather than the room.
- **Free** from Alia or Hesk. She is the fastest route to everything under this house and she will
  trade all of it for the children.
- **The door is stone on a pivot: Strength DC 18** to swing if the catch is not found first
  (**Disable Device DC 20**), and either way it grinds. **Nobody opens this quietly by force.**
- The initiates behind it are not expecting anyone. A party that finds the catch rather than
  forcing the door starts the tunnel fight on its own terms.
