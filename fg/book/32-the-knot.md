The slaves' junction, where five of their tunnels meet: a dug chamber seven squares by eight with
the roof held up by props thick as a leg. The Labyrinth uses it as a guard post and has drawn a
chalk maze on the floor, a good one, patiently done, round and round to a centre with nothing in it.

> Two young men in mail stand by a crate with a lantern on it, and a cord runs along the north wall
> above their heads and away down the west tunnel. One of them has his hand on the cord and the
> other has his hand on the first one's wrist. "Two," one says. "One," says the other. "It's his
> bell." A third man sits against the wall with a black chin-beard and a bloody bandage round his
> middle, watching the stairs, not them.

**Wendel and Lutz** came here on the Horn's order and are arguing about which code to ring. It does
not matter which they ring. See the bell, on the Low Road page.

**Chin Whiskers** is the scrapper who ran from the house with two crossbow bolts in him. He went
down through the Tanner's trapdoor, told the Keep what he had seen, and has been sitting here since
because nobody told him to do anything else.

- **He switches sides for a bandage.** A Heal check, a potion or a cure spell, and he tells them
  everything he knows about the house and the Keep, which is a good deal about the first and very
  little about the second.
- **He wants to know one thing:** whether Alia and the children are alive. Tell him the truth.

**Without a fight:**

- **Pass as Hesk's new porters.** The Keeper hires carriers through Hesk and nobody down here knows
  their faces. **Bluff DC 12**, **+5 if somebody is carrying a sack**, and the goblin is an asset,
  because Hesk has sent odder help before.
- **Give the order.** Whoever sounds most like a Master, told to stand aside, they stand aside.
- **Intimidate DC 11**, as in the lodging. They heard the house fight too.

**Where it goes:** east to the lodging; north to the grain store (C4); south into the old ways (C6);
west along the hall to the gate (C9). The swallow at the west mouth points west. The chalk maze
beside it points back the way you came.
