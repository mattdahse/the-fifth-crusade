# E2. The Kitchen

<!-- chapter: The Sarkorian Manor -->
<!-- section: The Manor -->
<!-- order: 2 -->
North-west corner: brick oven, fallen shelving, and the warmest room left in the building. One of
the two flues that the chimneys outside belong to.

**The two men live here now**, because it is warm, and because it is as far from the hatch as the
house goes. Two bedrolls by the oven, a pot, a cracked jug, and a hooked iron disc each on a thong
that neither of them wears any more. On the alarm they come out into the hall and plug the
doorways, because that is what they are told to do.

**They can be talked down** by anybody who offers a way out that does not go through the hatch,
and they will tell the company everything, including how long the tunnel is.
