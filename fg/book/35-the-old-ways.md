# C6. The Old Ways

<!-- chapter: The South Bank Job -->

South from the knot a narrower tunnel, two squares wide, runs down to a four-way split. These are
the slaves' oldest workings: the branches toward the houses on the South Bank where the slaves
were quartered, each ending at a trapdoor they could come and go by. The Labyrinth pays Emmet Rook
to clear them toward the street, a loaf a yard, and has cleared one.

**The four ways from the split:**

- **East, to the Tanner's.** Cleared, a couple of fallen stones, and a short wooden ladder up to a
  trapdoor in the floor of an abandoned tannery on Cinder Row, two doors down from the scrapyard.
  **This is the way Chin Whiskers came down.** It is also the party's way out to the street, and a
  roofed, empty building to rest in, with a door that bars.
- **South-east, to the Well House.** Into a dug chamber strewn with husks, where the centipedes nest,
  then a squeeze choked with fallen stone (**Escape Artist DC 10**, or a round of squeezing for a
  Small creature and two for a Medium one) to a round stone-lined shaft. **Climb DC 15**, thirty feet,
  to the bottom of a dry well in an empty yard.
- **South-west, to the fall** (C7), where the Bakehouse branch came down forty years ago.
- **West, the midden link**, two squares wide and straight, to the midden (C8).

**Three giant centipedes** nest in the chamber off the south-east branch.

> The chamber floor crunches. It is ankle-deep in dry husks the colour of old varnish, some of them
> as long as your arm, and against the far wall is a wooden crate with a sliding lid, a length of
> string tied to the lid, and a heap of small bones inside it.

**That is Jory Tallow's trap.** He baited it with rats and caught his centipedes here, the ones he let
loose in Lupenor's Market. A party that has met Jory knows whose it is the moment they see it.

- **Without a fight:** Emmet Rook takes them the long way round the nest and they never meet the
  centipedes. Smoke drives them into the squeeze. They are vermin, and they are hungry, and a
  dead rat thrown the other way buys a round.
- **The bite** carries the centipede's poison. See its record.

**The swallows** are on every branch, and on the east and south-east ones they point *back*, toward
the knot: those branches only ever went up into the city, and the way out was always west.
