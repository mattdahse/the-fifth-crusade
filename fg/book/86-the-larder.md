# G3. The Larder

<!-- chapter: The Sarkorian Manor -->
<!-- section: The Warren -->
<!-- order: 3 -->

Where the workers carry everything that does not belong in the warren: bones, rags, husks, a boot,
a broken shovel, a Labyrinth disc on a thong, and, in time, anything the company drops.

**Anything the workers have tidied away ends up here**, including unwedged charges and the torches
that lit them. A lit charge carried here and left goes off here, harmlessly as far as the queen is
concerned, which is a lesson worth one charge.

Among the bones are the eleven men off Ysolde's muster list. Most of them are not in one piece.
