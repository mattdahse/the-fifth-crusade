# Payment and Downtime

<!-- chapter: The South Bank Job -->
<!-- section: Afterwards -->
<!-- order: 1 -->

Mira pays at the end of Cinder Row, where she has been waiting in a hooded cloak that makes her more
conspicuous rather than less. She wants to hear **everything**, in order, twice. Her people take
any prisoners off their hands and ask no questions in front of them; Mira asks all of them.

Then she hands over the purse with a small ceremony, and **pitches the name again**: whatever the
party suggested last time, improved with an unnecessary word, and three new ones of her own. The
candidates for this meeting are on her record.

**150 gp**, a writ of the Market, and fifty more held with a moneylender. The coin is meant to be
spent at 2nd level rather than carried into the Marchlands - let the downtime happen.

Then she sends for them again, with the map on the table between her and them.
