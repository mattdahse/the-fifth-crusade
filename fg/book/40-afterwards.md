# Payment and Downtime

<!-- chapter: The Southshore Job -->
<!-- section: Afterwards -->
<!-- order: 1 -->

Mira pays at the end of Cinder Row, out of a purse she does not take out of her coat until the
party has stopped talking. Her people take any prisoners off their hands and ask no questions in
front of them.

**150 gp**, a writ of the Market, and fifty more held with a moneylender. The coin is meant to be
spent at 2nd level rather than carried into the Marchlands - let the downtime happen.

Then she sends for them again, with the map on the table between her and them.
