# Constrict (Ex)

<!-- id: darkmantle_constrict -->
<!-- abilitytype: Ex -->

A darkmantle that begins its turn **grappling** a creature deals **1d4+4** damage automatically,
with no attack roll, and again every round it maintains the grapple.

## At the table

Four to eight automatic damage a round, from something that has already wrapped a head, against
1st-level characters with eight to eleven hit points. **Two rounds in a darkmantle's grip kills
an average wizard**, and there is no attack roll to miss.

So the fight is decided by how quickly the *other* characters free the grappled one — and by
whether anybody kept a hand free before the ceiling moved.

Putting the lights out does not help. **Blindsight 90 ft.** means darkness changes nothing for
the darkmantle and everything for the party, which is worth saying out loud the first time
somebody reaches for a lantern.
