# Voice of the Brood (Su)

<!-- id: voice_of_the_brood -->
<!-- abilitytype: Su -->

While **Nib** is within **120 feet** of the young queen or her clutch, the fiendish vermin of this
hive treat her commands as though they came from the queen. Once per round, as a **free action**,
she can direct any number of soldiers she can see or hear: attack this, come here, dig there,
leave that alone. They obey.

This is **not** a mind-affecting effect. The ants are mindless and immune to those. They are not
being persuaded. As far as they can tell, she *is* part of the queen.

## At the table

**This is the secret of the house, and the ants are the clue.**

- **Ysolde gives the orders aloud and believes the crown carries them.** The ants obey only
  because Nib, invisible on her shoulder, repeats them. A careful party will notice that the ants
  respond a moment *after* Ysolde speaks, and sometimes to things she did not quite say.
- **Take Nib away and the crown stops working.** Kill her, drive her off, or catch her in a
  ***protection from evil*** that keeps her from touching Ysolde, and the next order Ysolde gives
  is met by nothing at all. Play that silence long. It is the moment the whole adventure is built
  around.
- **Nib can turn the ants on Ysolde.** If it suits her (Ysolde is losing, or has become a
  liability), one whispered word sends the soldiers for the acolyte instead. Ysolde will still be
  giving orders when they reach her.
- **The queen herself is not commanded.** She lays and guards her clutch and does not take
  direction from anyone, which is why the sealed room is the one place in the house Nib is careful
  about.
