# Call the Brood (Ex)

<!-- id: call_the_brood -->
<!-- abilitytype: Ex -->

As a **standard action**, the queen shrills through her mandibles at a pitch that carries down
every tunnel of the nest. **1d3 giant ant soldiers** arrive from the bore-hole in the chamber
floor at the start of the queen's next turn.

She can do this **once**, and she does it immediately.

## At the table

This is the dial that sets the difficulty of the boss fight, and it should be set on purpose
rather than rolled blind:

- **Roll it honestly (1d3)** if the party came in fresh, cleared the cellar first, and is at 2nd
  level. That is the fight as designed.
- **Fix it at 1** if they have already fought both factions today, or are still 1st level. The
  queen alone is already a serious encounter at CR 4.
- **Fix it at 3** only if the party has been careful, is rested, and has thinned the nest below
  already — and even then, expect somebody to go down.

**If the party has burned the clutch in the cellar**, no soldiers come. Say why out loud when it
happens: there is nothing left down there to answer her. That is the payoff for having done the
cellar properly, and it should feel like one.
