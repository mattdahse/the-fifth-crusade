# Poison (Ex)

<!-- id: poison_giant_ant -->
<!-- abilitytype: Ex -->

**Sting &#8212; injury.** *Save* Fortitude DC 14, negates. *Frequency* 1/round for 4 rounds.
*Effect* 1d2 Strength damage. *Cure* 1 save.

The save DC is Constitution-based, and is the one on this creature's own sting — a different ant
in a different nest will not have the same number.

## At the table

Strength damage, not hit points, so it does not show up on the tracker as anything dramatic and
that is exactly why it hurts. Four rounds of 1d2 is an average of six Strength off a character
who probably started with fourteen, and every point of it is attack rolls, damage, and carrying
what they came down here to carry back up.

**One successful save ends it**, so most victims shrug it off in a round or two. The danger is
the character who fails the first two and is now fighting at &#8211;2 to hit with an ant holding
their arm.

Worth tracking honestly rather than hand-waving: this poison is the reason a party wins the fight
and still has to decide whether they can afford to keep going.
