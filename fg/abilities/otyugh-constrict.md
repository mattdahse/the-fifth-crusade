# Constrict (Ex)

<!-- id: otyugh_constrict -->
<!-- abilitytype: Ex -->

When the Bishop succeeds on a grapple check against a creature it holds with a tentacle, it deals
**1d6+2** bludgeoning damage in addition to anything else the check does. It gets this damage each
round it maintains the hold, with no attack roll.

## At the table

A tentacle that hits grabs, and a grabbed character is pulled toward the pit and squeezed every
round after. Escaping is a grapple or Escape Artist check against **CMD 19**, which a 1st-level
character mostly cannot make.

So the answer is the one the slaves found: **feed it**. A held character stops being held the moment
the Bishop is offered something it would rather eat.
