# Poison (Ex)

<!-- id: poison_giant_centipede -->
<!-- abilitytype: Ex -->

**Bite &#8212; injury.** *Save* Fortitude DC 13, negates. *Frequency* 1/round for 6 rounds.
*Effect* 1d3 Dexterity damage. *Cure* 1 save.

The save DC is Constitution-based and includes a +2 racial bonus. It is this centipede's number,
not every centipede's.

## At the table

Dexterity damage, not hit points. A character who fails two saves in a row loses AC, Reflex saves
and ranged attack bonus, and a Dexterity-based fighter loses their melee bonus as well. One
successful save ends it, so most victims shake it off in a round or two.

**On a bystander it is the bite that matters, not the poison.** Most of the people in the
market have three or four hit points and a bite averages two and a half, so one bite drops about
half of them and a second finishes the job. Pip and Old Hrenna have two, and one bite is enough. The poison then keeps working on somebody already lying in the street. That is
where a healer's attention goes in this fight, and it is why a *stabilize* or a *cure* on a
stallholder is worth as much here as any hit on a centipede.
