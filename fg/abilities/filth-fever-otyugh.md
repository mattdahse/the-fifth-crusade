# Disease: Filth Fever (Ex)

<!-- id: filth_fever_otyugh -->
<!-- abilitytype: Ex -->

**Bite, injury.** Fortitude **DC 14**. Onset 1d3 days. Frequency 1/day. Effect 1d3 Dex damage and
1d3 Con damage. Cure: two consecutive saves.

## At the table

It does nothing tonight and a great deal on the third day. Anyone bitten should be told they were
bitten by something that lives in a midden, and left to draw their own conclusions about the wound.
