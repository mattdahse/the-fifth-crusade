# Fiendish Resistances (Ex)

<!-- id: fiendish_resistances -->
<!-- abilitytype: Ex -->

From the **fiendish** template. A creature with 4 Hit Dice or fewer gains **resistance 5 to cold
and resistance 5 to fire**, and its alignment shifts to evil. Damage reduction and spell
resistance come later, at 5 Hit Dice and above, so a creature this small has neither.

## At the table

Resistance 5 is small on paper and large at 1st level, because it is subtracted from every
individual hit rather than from the total. A *burning hands* that rolls 4 fire damage does
nothing at all. An alchemist's fire does one point.

This is deliberately the wrong monster to burn. The party's most obvious answers to a swarm of
vermin — fire, and more fire — are the two energy types that land softest here, and a table that
works that out and switches to blunt weapons and the doorway has earned the fight.
