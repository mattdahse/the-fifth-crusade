# Grab (Ex)

<!-- id: grab -->
<!-- abilitytype: Ex -->

If the creature hits with the listed attack, it can start a grapple as a **free action** without
provoking an attack of opportunity. It does not need a free hand, and it takes no penalty to the
combat manoeuvre check.

While it holds a grapple it keeps its **Dexterity bonus to AC** rather than losing it, and it can
choose to take a **&#8211;20 penalty** on the grapple check in order to keep using the rest of its
body normally — for a giant ant, that means holding a victim in its mandibles and stinging them
with the tail in the same round.

Escaping needs a successful **grapple check** or an **Escape Artist** check against the creature's
CMD.

## At the table

Grab is what turns one bite into a fight the party cannot walk away from. A grabbed character is
not going anywhere on their turn, the ant does not have to chase them, and a second ant will
happily bite something that cannot dodge.

The **&#8211;20 option** is the one worth remembering. An ant that has hold of the front-rank
fighter can still sting them each round, and giving up the grapple bonus costs it almost nothing
because it never intended to pin.
