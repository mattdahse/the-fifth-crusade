# Poison (Ex)

<!-- id: poison_ant_queen -->
<!-- abilitytype: Ex -->

**Sting &#8212; injury.** *Save* Fortitude DC 18, negates. *Frequency* 1/round for 6 rounds.
*Effect* 1d3 Strength damage. *Cure* 1 save.

Constitution-based, and hers alone — the soldiers' sting is a much weaker DC 14 for 1d2 over four
rounds. This is the same poison grown up.

## At the table

At 1st or 2nd level a DC 18 Fortitude save is a coin-flip for the fighter and a near-certainty of
failure for everybody else. Six rounds of 1d3 will take a lightly-built character from functional
to barely able to lift a weapon, and unlike hit-point damage there is no healing it in the fight.

That is deliberate: **the queen's poison is a clock.** A party that trades blows with her patiently
will find themselves weaker every round while she is not. The answer is not to out-last her.
