# Disease: Filth Fever (Ex)

<!-- id: filth_fever_dire_rat -->
<!-- abilitytype: Ex -->

**Bite, injury.** Fortitude **DC 11**. Onset 1d3 days. Frequency 1/day. Effect 1d3 Dex damage and
1d3 Con damage. Cure: two consecutive saves.

## At the table

Easy to shake with a decent Fortitude save, and easy to forget. A bite in the grain store is worth
a note on the GM's side, because the fever comes on in the days after the job, when the party is
spending Mira's money.
