# The Outfitter's Counter, Lupenor's Market

<!-- id: lupenors_counter -->
<!-- markup: 1.2 -->

Lupenor's trading house keeps a counter at the back of the market yard for people going out
into the Marchlands, and **Mira Thistledance's people send everyone they hire to it first**.

Nothing here is fine. Drezen has been in crusader hands for weeks, not years, and everything on
these shelves either came up the road from Kenabres on a cart or came out of the city's own
ruins and was made honest again. **Prices run about a fifth over the book** — that is not
gouging, it is what a city with one road and no workshops charges.

The counter does not stock magic. For that the party wants somebody with a name, and they do not
have one yet.
