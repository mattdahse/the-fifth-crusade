**James Helmsmith** came up out of Kenabres with his shop in crates and a goblin apprentice, and
set both up again in Drezen. He is an alchemist and a shopkeeper. He was an adventurer once, and
he still talks about reagents like a man who used to gather his own. The shelves smell of
vinegar, tallow and something sharper underneath it. Anything that might go off sits on the high
shelves, where a customer's elbow won't reach it.

Everything here is made at the bench in the back room or brought up the road from Kenabres. Like
everything else in Drezen it costs about a fifth over the book.
