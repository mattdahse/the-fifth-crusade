# The Scrapyard Strongbox

<!-- id: scrapyard_strongbox -->

Bolted through the floor under the shop counter, behind a hinged board that looks like part of the
counter until somebody pulls on it. An iron box the size of a bread crock.

**Finding it** is a search of the counter rather than of the shop. **Opening it** is Hesk's neck
key, a Disable Device check against a good lock, or an hour with a crowbar and no one listening.
A scrap dealer in an occupied city kept his money where he could sit on it.
