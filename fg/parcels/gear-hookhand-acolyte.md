Iron, lashed over the left hand with cord that has worn grooves into the wrist. It comes off; it
has not been off in a long while. As a weapon it is poor. As an argument it has never needed to
be used twice.

<!-- type: Gear -->

A roll of oiled cloth holding a bone stylus, three stubs of chalk, a flask of lamp oil, and a
palm-sized slate scored with a maze. She prepares from the slate each morning and it means
nothing to anyone else.
