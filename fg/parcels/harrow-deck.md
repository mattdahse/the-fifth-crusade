# A Harrow Deck

<!-- id: harrow_deck -->

A whole deck, fifty-four cards, for when one turns up as loot or a gift rather than as one of
Mira's cut five. The five she hands out are [`harrow-cards-mira.md`](harrow-cards-mira.md).

The SRD prices a harrow deck at 100 gp and lists no weight at all; a weightless item breaks
encumbrance silently, so the weight below is ours — fifty-four pasteboard cards and a wrap, near
enough a pound.

## Items

### Harrow Deck
<!-- srd: none -->
<!-- count: 1 -->
<!-- type: Gear -->
<!-- cost: 100 gp -->
<!-- weight: 1 -->

A fortune-teller's deck of fifty-four cards, each one hand-painted on heavy pasteboard inside a
border of worn gilt, the backs a plain indigo. The corners are soft and the gilt is worn through
along the edges where the deck has been squared a great many times. It sits on a square of dark red
cloth and ties shut with a plaited cord. Nothing about it is new, and nothing about it is valuable except
the painting.
