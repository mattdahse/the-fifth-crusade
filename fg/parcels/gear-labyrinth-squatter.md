Brown with rust through most of its scales and missing a handful along the left flank. Sound
enough to turn an edge, heavy enough to drown in, and it stinks of the gambeson underneath.
Worth a quarter of the book price to anyone honest, and rather more to someone who needs armour
tonight.

### Hooked Labyrinth Token
<!-- count: 1 -->
<!-- type: Gear -->
<!-- cost: 1 gp -->
<!-- weight: 0.5 -->
<!-- nonid: A hooked iron disc, stamped with a maze -->

The Ivory Labyrinth's mark on a leather thong, worn against the skin. Not a holy symbol and no
use as one. It is a pass — it says the wearer is owed food and a roof at any house that keeps the
mark, and it is the only reason he is still alive this far north.
