### The Crown of the Swarm
<!-- count: 1 -->
<!-- type: Gear -->
<!-- cost: 5 gp -->
<!-- weight: 1 -->
<!-- nonid: A copper circlet with two brass antennae on springs -->

A band of beaten copper, green at the edges, with two long brass antennae on coiled springs that
bob when the wearer nods. The inside of the band is lined with felt, worn through, and stamped
with the mark of a Sarkorian mummers' guild: a costume crown, for the Locust King in a harvest
play, older than anybody now living. It is not magic and never was.

### Adept's Kit
<!-- count: 1 -->
<!-- type: Gear -->
<!-- cost: 8 gp -->
<!-- weight: 4 -->

A roll of oiled cloth holding a bone stylus, three stubs of chalk, a flask of lamp oil, and a
palm-sized slate. The slate is scored with a locust, wings spread, cut over the top of an older
mark that was a maze. She prepares from it each morning. The lamp oil is for the lamp — she has
never thrown it at anyone and will not start now.

### Folded Muster List
<!-- count: 1 -->
<!-- type: Gear -->
<!-- cost: 0 gp -->
<!-- weight: 0.1 -->
<!-- nonid: A folded square of vellum, much handled -->

Nineteen names in a careful hand. **Eleven are struck through.** Two more are crossed out with a
different mark and fresh ink, with the word *deserted* beside them: **Garth Wyle** and **Tam
Ricket**.

She struck the eleven herself. Every man on this list who argued with her about the hatch went
down it. The eleventh strike is three weeks old, and it is the same week the tally ledger stops
counting crusaders.

Read together with the ledger this is the whole case against her, in her own handwriting, and it
is worth a great deal to **Mira Thistledance**, who has a whole corner of her wall of red string
given over to why the Labyrinth's counting-post went quiet without being taken.
