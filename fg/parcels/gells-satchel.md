# Treasure: Gell's Satchel

<!-- id: gells_satchel -->

**Up the cold flue of the great hall's hearth.** A waxed leather satchel wedged on a ledge of
brick about six feet up the chimney, where a Tiny flier could put it and nobody standing on the
floor would ever look. Nib hid it there the week Gell died, meaning to come back for it once she
had worked out what was worth anything. She never quite has.

**Finding it:** **Perception DC 15** at the hearth notices that the soot on the hearthstones is
printed all over with **small clawed tracks**, like a large bird's, going in and out of the flue.
After that, anyone who reaches up the chimney finds it. A *detect magic* cast in the hall shows a
faint glow in the chimney breast. Nib will also give it up, last of everything, in a bargain.

### Ostrander Gell's Spellbook

A wizard's travelling spellbook, slim and much used, in black calfskin with the Ivory Labyrinth's
maze tooled into the cover. Inside, in a small, precise hand:

- **1st level:** *alarm, comprehend languages, grease, mage armor, protection from evil, silent
  image, unseen servant*
- **2nd level:** *glitterdust, see invisibility*

**The margins are a diary**, and the last pages are the reason to read it. Gell's notes on *the
specimen under the house* go from scholarly to excited over a few weeks. Then, three pages from
the end, in the same hand: *N. lies about the ants. They turn when she turns. Must speak to Y.
before N. does.* The page after that is the last. It is about the best place to stand to observe
the queen, and it says that N. has offered to show him.
