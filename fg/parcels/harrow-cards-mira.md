# Mira's Harrow Cards

<!-- id: harrow_cards_mira -->

The five cards Mira Thistledance brings to the first meeting. Four she presses on the company as
their "operative cards"; the fifth she keeps for herself. Cut from a harrow deck she did not buy
whole — the rest of the deck is elsewhere, and she has never said where.

They carry no magic and are worth nothing. They are identification, in the sense that a thing is
identification when one enthusiastic half-elf has decided it is.

Each card is its own item so a player can hold their own and the rest can go where they like.

## Items

### The Wanderer

<!-- weight: 0.1 -->

A single harrow card, pasteboard worn soft at the corners, painted with a lone figure walking away
down an empty road under a wide pale sky. A book is worked small into each corner of the border.
The back is plain indigo. Someone has pressed a thumb into the lower edge often enough to darken it.

### The Mountain Man
<!-- srd: none -->
<!-- count: 1 -->
<!-- type: Gear -->
<!-- cost: 0 gp -->
<!-- weight: 0.1 -->

A single harrow card, painted with a broad hooded figure climbing a snowbound pass on a staff, a
ragged red cloak blowing out behind, bare peaks closing on both sides. A round shield is worked
small into each corner of the border. The back is plain indigo, with a crease across one corner
where it has been folded and flattened again.

### The Juggler
<!-- srd: none -->
<!-- count: 1 -->
<!-- type: Gear -->
<!-- cost: 0 gp -->
<!-- weight: 0.1 -->

A single harrow card, painted with a pair of raised open hands and five things caught in the air
above them — a vial, a coin, a knife, an egg and a bright spark — the juggler's face out of frame. A
key is worked small into each corner of the border. The back is plain indigo. The face is lightly
scuffed, as though it has been shuffled more than the others.

### The Vision
<!-- srd: none -->
<!-- count: 1 -->
<!-- type: Gear -->
<!-- cost: 0 gp -->
<!-- weight: 0.1 -->

A single harrow card, painted with a seated figure in a deep red robe, hands open in the lap, the
eyes bound in pale cloth with a second pair of eyes drawn open on the cloth itself, a rayed halo
behind the head. A book is worked small into each corner of the border. The back is plain indigo.

### The Theater
<!-- srd: none -->
<!-- count: 1 -->
<!-- type: Gear -->
<!-- cost: 0 gp -->
<!-- weight: 0.1 -->

A single harrow card, painted with a lit and empty stage seen from the wings, a half-drawn curtain,
two masks left face-up on the boards. A crown is worked small into each corner of the border. The
back is plain indigo. This one has been handled most of all; the gilt on the border is worn through
to the pasteboard along one side.
