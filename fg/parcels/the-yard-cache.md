# The Yard Cache

<!-- id: yard_cache -->

Not hidden from thieves. Hidden from whoever comes with a writ, which in this quarter has always
been the more likely visitor.

**Where.** Under the chopping block, which does not sit flat and never has. Lift it and there is a
tarred canvas bundle in a hole lined with a barrel end.

**Finding it** takes deliberate searching — a Perception check made while actually looking at the
yard rather than crossing it, or one clever question put to Sera, who has watched her father move
it twice. **A party that never searches the yard never finds this**, and that is the intent: the
yard reads as scenery and it is not.
