# The Dressing Room

<!-- id: the_dressing_room -->

Where arrivals off the ladder are given city clothes and a name before they are walked up through
the scrapyard (C12). The register is the second most important document in the Low Road.

## Items

### The Arrivals Register
<!-- srd: none -->
<!-- count: 1 -->
<!-- type: Gear -->
<!-- cost: 0 gp -->
<!-- weight: 1 -->
<!-- nonid: A narrow book of entries in a clerk's hand -->

A narrow book in a careful clerk's hand: fourteen entries since Rova. For each one, a date, the name
the arrival was given, the trade that went with it, and a remark. The remarks are short and not
kind: *a cooper; cannot cooper.* *A laundress; for the market end.* *A widow, for the west end;
comes and goes by the ladder.* The eighth and the eleventh entries read only *For the Preservers*,
with no remark at all.
