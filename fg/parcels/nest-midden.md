# Gear: The Nest Midden

<!-- id: nest_midden -->

A drone carries nothing, so this stands in for its gear: what is recoverable from the nest floor
once the biting stops. Everything here came off something the hive ate.

## Coin

- 31 GP
- 96 SP

## Items

### Chitin Plate
<!-- count: 4 -->
<!-- type: Gear -->
<!-- cost: 15 gp -->
<!-- weight: 3 -->
<!-- nonid: A curved plate of dark red shell, oddly cold -->

Cut from a drone's back. It stays cold in a warm room and it does not take a stain. An armourer
who works the Worldwound salvage trade will pay for it and not ask; anyone else will want to know
where it came from before they touch it.

### Crusader's Signet, Corroded
<!-- count: 1 -->
<!-- type: Gear -->
<!-- cost: 40 gp -->
<!-- weight: 0 -->
<!-- nonid: A pitted silver ring, the device eaten away -->

Silver, and the device on the face has been chewed to nothing by seventy years in the wet. The
band still carries three letters inside. Somebody walked into this cellar a long time before the
party did, and did not walk out.

### Sacks, Ruined
<!-- count: 2 -->
<!-- type: Gear -->
<!-- cost: 0 gp -->
<!-- weight: 1 -->

Waxed sailcloth, gnawed through, hauled down here from the cellar above. The wax has kept the
stencil legible, and it is a Kenabres quartermaster's mark — which means the hive has been into
the cellar's stores, and means somebody was still supplying this house within living memory.
