# The Nest Midden

<!-- id: nest_midden -->

**Not a gear parcel.** The ants carry nothing and wear nothing, so nothing here rides on the NPC
record — this is what is recoverable from the nest floor once the biting stops, and it belongs to
the room. Linked from [the encounter](../encounters/the-ant-nest.md) rather than from the
creatures. Everything in it came off something the hive ate.

## Items

### Chitin Plate
<!-- count: 4 -->
<!-- type: Gear -->
<!-- cost: 15 gp -->
<!-- weight: 3 -->
<!-- nonid: A curved plate of dark red shell, oddly cold -->

Cut from a soldier's back. It stays cold in a warm room and it does not take a stain. An armourer
who works the Worldwound salvage trade will pay for it and not ask; anyone else will want to know
where it came from before they touch it.

### Arcanist's Signet
<!-- count: 1 -->
<!-- type: Gear -->
<!-- cost: 40 gp -->
<!-- weight: 0.1 -->
<!-- nonid: A silver signet ring, a maze cut in its face -->

Silver, with the Ivory Labyrinth's maze cut deep in the face and three letters inside the band:
*O. G.* It was on a finger bone, among a scatter of other bones too big to be an ant's dinner and
too few to be a whole man, wrapped in the rags of a good grey robe. **Ostrander Gell**, who ran
this post, and who came down here a year ago to study the queen.

### Sacks, Ruined
<!-- count: 2 -->
<!-- type: Gear -->
<!-- cost: 0 gp -->
<!-- weight: 1 -->

Waxed sailcloth, gnawed through, hauled down here from the cellar above. The wax has kept the
stencil legible, and it is a Kenabres quartermaster's mark — which means the hive has been into
the cellar's stores, and means somebody was still supplying this house within living memory.
