# Mira's Payment

<!-- id: miras_payment -->

Paid at the end of Cinder Row, out of a purse she does not take out of her coat until the party
has stopped talking. She counts it into a hand rather than putting it down.

## Coin

- 150 GP

## Items

### Writ of the Market
<!-- srd: none -->
<!-- count: 1 -->
<!-- type: Gear -->
<!-- cost: 0 gp -->
<!-- weight: 0 -->
<!-- nonid: A folded paper with a merchant's seal, no name on the outside -->

Not a commission and not a licence. It says the bearer trades on behalf of Lupenor's Market and
asks that they be given passage and a fair price, and it is signed by a factor whose name means
nothing outside Drezen and a great deal inside it.

It is the first thing anybody has given these four that says they belong to something.

### Letter of Credit, Drezen
<!-- srd: none -->
<!-- count: 1 -->
<!-- type: Gear -->
<!-- cost: 50 gp -->
<!-- weight: 0 -->
<!-- nonid: A sealed note on good paper -->

Fifty gold held with a Drezen moneylender against a name the bearer gives when they collect it.
Mira wrote it that way deliberately: the coin in hand is for spending, and this is for coming
back to.
