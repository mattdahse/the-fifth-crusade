# The Counting Room

<!-- id: the_counting_room -->

What is on and under the Keeper's desk (C10). The packed bag is under the cot, and it is the
object that ends the quarrel.

## Coin

- 52 GP

## Items

### The Keeper's Ledger
<!-- srd: none -->
<!-- count: 1 -->
<!-- type: Gear -->
<!-- cost: 0 gp -->
<!-- weight: 3 -->
<!-- nonid: A clerk's ledger, ruled in five columns -->

A ledger in a careful clerk's hand, ruled in five columns: *Received*, *Sent West*, *Spoiled*, *The
Bishop* and *Shrinkage*. Every column adds up. *Spoiled* and *Sent West* rise together, week on
week, by the same amounts. An entry in Lamashan reads: *Helmsmith's, potions, four, bought, two
hundred gold.* The newest entry, in fresh ink, reads: *Arrivals, unscheduled: four.*

Three letters in the same careful hand, each sealed with a maze and addressed to the Templars of
the Ivory Labyrinth, each asking for a ruling on who holds the title of Master of the Drezen Way.
The third is shorter than the first.

### On Mines and Countermines
<!-- srd: none -->

<!-- type: Gear -->

A treatise on siege-mining in a Kenabres printing, water-stained along the bottom edge: sapping,
shoring, countermines and the proper spacing of props, with engraved plates of the tools. Somebody
pressed a dead moth at the chapter on shoring and never came back for it.
